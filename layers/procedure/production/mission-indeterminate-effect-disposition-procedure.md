## Entry Condition

Enter only when Runtime durably records:

```text
an exact dispatched mission effect
+ provider outcome INDETERMINATE
+ exact effect, attempt, mission, deployment, and operative-binding references
+ effect quarantine
```

Timeout alone is insufficient unless it produces the cited indeterminate observation.

## Expected Sequence

### 1. Preserve And Withhold

1. Preserve the Runtime observation and PB-001 lineage.
2. Keep the exact effect quarantined.
3. Withhold repeat, compensation, replacement, or dependent effects.
4. Do not infer provider refusal, completion, mission failure, or success.

### 2. Admit The Observation

1. Treat the Runtime envelope as an internal operational observation eligible for Curia's admitted observation input.
2. Route any provider-returned content carried with or cited by the envelope through Lazaretto before Curia use.
3. Lazaretto preserves or quarantines external material without deciding outcome.
4. The Chief of Staff admits the safe envelope and any sanitized provider material to the matching Curia Session and Situation Picture.
5. Exact-correlation mismatch blocks use.

Runtime telemetry does not become external field material merely because it describes an external-effect boundary.

### 3. Reconcile Knowable Facts

The Chief of Staff assembles:

- the dispatched request and effect identity
- Runtime attempt and transport observations
- applicable provider intervention ledger stages
- any provider result or later return
- current mission state and dependent actions
- applicable grants and their current status
- known inability to establish outcome

Absence of a provider stage is recorded as absence, not converted into failure.

### 4. Executive Disposition

The CEO may decide only with an effective Executive Mandate and applicable Mission Envelope authority.

Disposition classes:

```text
WAIT_FOR_OBSERVATION
REQUEST_NON_EFFECTING_STATUS_CHECK
PRESERVE_EFFECT_AS_INDETERMINATE
AUTHORIZE_SAFE_REPEAT
AUTHORIZE_COMPENSATING_EFFECT
ALTER_OR_TERMINATE_DEPENDENT_MISSION_PATH
ESCALATE_THROUGH_AN_ADMITTED_ROUTE
```

These labels classify possible next procedural paths; they do not create Authority action classes.

A disposition is available only when its native Authority, capability, competence, and artifact requirements exist. This procedure does not manufacture them.

### 5. Operationalize

1. The Chief of Staff records and hands the authorized disposition to the matching Muster instance.
2. Muster produces only the exact authorized instruction.
3. Runtime rechecks Authority, PB-001 correlation, Procedure permission, effect identity, and current quarantine state immediately before any consequential dispatch.
4. Any new indeterminate effect re-enters this procedure under its own exact effect identity.

### 6. Exit

Exit when one of these externally defined conditions is recorded:

- provider outcome becomes sufficiently established for the mission's native assessment
- an authorized safe repeat or compensation completes operationally and returns for assessment
- the CEO preserves indeterminacy and selects an authorized dependent-path disposition
- the mission enters an admitted wind-down or terminal path
- Authority becomes unavailable and the exact effect remains quarantined

Exit does not itself establish mission success, completion, closure, release, or provider truth.

## Prohibited Inference

```text
timeout ≠ failure
acknowledgement ≠ effect completion
status check ≠ permission to alter provider state
safe technical repeat ≠ authorized repeat
compensation capability ≠ compensation authority
effect outcome known ≠ mission outcome known
quarantine ≠ mission closure
```

## Control-Plane Boundary

This procedure does not cover an indeterminate control-plane effect.

Master Mason owns bounded control-plane diagnosis and the Runtime Maintenance Procedure orders withholding, safe-state preservation, reassessment, and escalation. A structural or semantic-risk disposition remains with the external operator under exact Authority.

Therefore control-plane indeterminacy has a separate bounded path; it is not an unresolved responsibility that this mission procedure must absorb.

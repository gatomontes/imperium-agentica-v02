# Provider-Neutral Deployment Policy Convergence Contract

## Status

Draft B1.3 candidate only.

This contract does not bind a mission, issue a Tool or Access Grant, retrieve credentials, declare readiness, authorize initial crossing, invoke Runtime, or deploy anything.

## Native Ownership

Authority owns the convergence decision. Provenance supplies exact artifact identity, correlation, ordering, lineage, contest, and supersession evidence. Procedure may require the finding but cannot create it. Muster may consume it but cannot infer it from assembly completeness. Runtime and Iron Gate may later enforce an admitted projection but cannot originate it.

No new Deployment, Identity, Authentication, Assurance, Procedure, or Runtime layer is justified.

## Core Distinctions

```text
valid Deployment Authorization ≠ converged deployment policy
satisfied authentication requirement ≠ converged deployment policy
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ mission binding
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ Tool Grant
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ Access Grant
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ credential custody
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ READY_FOR_LAUNCH
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ INITIAL_EXTERNAL_CROSSING authority
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT ≠ deployment execution
```

## Core Question

Are one exact Deployment Authorization and every authentication-satisfaction finding required by it simultaneously current, mutually correlated, scope-compatible, and available as one exact provider-neutral prerequisite set?

## Convergence Subject

One assessment binds exact references to:

- Deployment Authorization identity and version
- represented Principal and competent Authority Basis
- authorized deployer identity
- exact Operative identity and version
- permitted action and deployment purpose
- target scope and environment
- Mission Identity and correlation when applicable
- effective interval and parent validity
- every authentication requirement identity and version
- every required authentication-proof presentation and satisfaction finding
- required Tool and Access Grant references when applicable, without revalidating them
- required Provenance findings
- known blockers, prohibitions, contests, and unresolved conditions

## Findings

```text
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_NOT_CONFORMANT
DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_UNRESOLVED
```

Conformant requires exact identity, version, subject, action, purpose, target, environment, mission correlation, interval, parent, authentication-requirement, satisfaction, prohibition, and Provenance alignment.

A known mismatch, prohibition, denial, expiry, withdrawal, revocation, compromise, replay, parent invalidation, or supersession yields not conformant.

Missing, stale, contested, unverifiable, ambiguously correlated, or partially evaluated material evidence yields unresolved.

Silence, technical capability, provider acceptance, credential possession, assembly completeness, and prior success never imply convergence.

## Atomic Availability Rule

The finding is available only while every cited prerequisite remains available.

```text
any required prerequisite unavailable
→ DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT unavailable
→ reassessment required
```

A later correction or repair creates a new assessment version. Historical findings remain preserved.

## Downstream Boundary

Procedure may require a current `DEPLOYMENT_POLICY_CONFORMANT` finding before a later assembly or crossing-eligibility transition.

Muster may cite it while assembling a mission-specific Deployment Package. It does not create an Operative Binding, provide tools or credentials, or establish `READY_FOR_LAUNCH`.

`INITIAL_EXTERNAL_CROSSING` remains a separate Authority action. The convergence finding cannot issue or replace it.

Runtime and Iron Gate may later reject absent, stale, mismatched, or nonconformant policy evidence. Enforcement does not create Authority.

## Non-Admissions

This draft admits no production semantic revision, live grant, live authentication, credential, custody, identity provider, secret store, protocol, credential format, provider, Runtime driver, deployment mechanism, mission binding, Deployment Package, readiness, initial crossing, activation, deployment, rollback, or external effect.

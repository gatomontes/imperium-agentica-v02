# CB-DENIED — Cognitive Candidates Not Admitted

## Status

CB-DENIED is the disposition surface for Cognitive Baseline candidates that were proposed but never admitted.

A denied candidate is not a current standard and must not be treated as a superseded baseline.

## Required Record

Each denied candidate should record:

- candidate identifier or source path;
- observed problem or proposed change;
- reason admission was denied;
- evidence relied upon;
- whether the candidate may be reconsidered;
- destination of retained supporting material.

## Distinction

- CB-CURRENT: active cognitive standard.
- CB-###: numbered admitted snapshot and historical progression record.
- CB-DENIED: proposed cognitive candidate that never became an admitted snapshot.
- Retired or superseded numbered snapshots remain historical; they are not denied.

No CB-DENIED record authorizes implementation, promotion, or external effect.

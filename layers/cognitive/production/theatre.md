# Theatre

## Status

Admitted.

Baseline: `CB-CURRENT`.

Admission: `Production Admission Review 003`.

Evidence: `Constitutional Test Run 015 — 30 PASS / 0 FAIL`.

This file defines the provisional Theatre for Imperium v02.

It does not admit a runtime implementation, live execution surface, external automation, or orchestration layer.

---

## Purpose

The Theatre is the mission execution terrain.

It is where a launched operative encounters the outside world, task environment, tools, constraints, uncertainty, and consequence.

Imperium may prepare operatives for the Theatre, but the Theatre is not the same as Imperium's internal production layer.

---

## Core Question

```text
What happened when the operative encountered mission terrain?
```

---

## Theatre Returns

The Theatre may produce or expose:

- operative reports
- artifacts
- observations
- evidence
- failures
- boundary contacts
- tool-use outcomes
- key/access outcomes
- mission results
- unexpected conditions
- final dossier material

---

## Relationships Across La Cortine

Muster prepares the mission-bound Deployment Package.

Iron Gate launches it through La Cortine into Theatre.

During execution, the deployed operative may present tool or access tickets through Barbican to Armory or Locksmith.

Completed, terminated, or failed mission returns enter Citadel through Lazaretto.

---

## Relationship To Lazaretto

Returns from Theatre go to Lazaretto.

The Lazaretto receives and preserves returns before judgment.

---

## Non-Authority

The Theatre must not be treated as:

- government
- judgment
- admission
- truth by itself
- self-justifying execution
- final report writer

---

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Theatre exposes consequence.
Lazaretto receives consequence.
Judicature evaluates consequence.
```

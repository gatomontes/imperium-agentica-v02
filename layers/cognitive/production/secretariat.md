# Secretariat

## Status

Admitted cognitive intake and delivery boundary.

Baseline: `CB-001`.

Admission: `Production Admission Review 003`.

Evidence: `Constitutional Test Run 015 — 30 PASS / 0 FAIL`.

Secretariat is Imperium's administrative point of entry and final delivery surface.

## Core Question

```text
What has the operator submitted, and what authorized artifact must be delivered back?
```

## Responsibilities

Secretariat:

- receives operator intent
- preserves the operator, request, timing, and supplied-material provenance
- shapes the intake into a Petition without adding mission meaning
- routes the Petition to Castellan
- receives the authorized Final Report or requested terminal artifact
- creates the Delivery Package
- delivers without changing substance
- records receipt and delivery status

## Products

```text
Petition
Delivery Package
administrative receipt and delivery record
```

## Non-Authority

Secretariat must not:

- decide doability or mission purpose
- resolve profession
- forge a persona or Officer
- recruit or deploy an operative
- grant tools, access, or authority
- judge Theatre returns
- choose mission disposition
- revise a Final Report or Curia decision

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Secretariat receives.
Castellan forms.
Secretariat delivers what authorized producers return.
```

# Curia Session Assignment

## Canonical Dependencies

## Status

Admitted cognitive assignment contract.

Baseline: `CB-CURRENT`.

Evidence: `Constitutional Test Run 016 — 31 PASS / 0 FAIL`.

A Curia Session Assignment binds one Preceptory-admitted Advisory Officer to one mission-specific Curia session for a bounded counsel need.

It replaces the unnecessary Commission concept.

## Core Distinctions

```text
Preceptory admission ≠ session assignment
standing Officer ≠ mission-dedicated staff
advisory role ≠ executive authority
Curia Session Assignment ≠ Standing Curia Assignment
Curia Session Assignment ≠ Executive Mandate
session end ≠ Officer deletion
```

## Formation

```text
counsel need
→ Chief of Staff engages Collegium
→ Collegium produces Advisory Role Requirement
→ Preceptory confirms suitable admitted Officer
→ Chief of Staff records Curia Session Assignment
→ Officer advises within scope
```

Collegium determines suitability. Preceptory supplies the admitted Officer record. The Chief of Staff binds that Officer to the active session as part of Curial Orchestration.

No separate commissioning protocol is required.

## Scope And Isolation

The assignment applies to exactly one Mission Identity and Curia Session identity.

The same Advisory Officer may serve another session only through a distinct assignment and only when standing-role and Officer capacity permit.

Advice, evidence, dissent, and conflicts remain correlated to the assigned session.

A session assignment cannot be copied across missions because the role appears similar.

## End

The assignment ends when:

- the counsel need is satisfied
- the Officer recuses or becomes unavailable
- the Chief of Staff ends the participation within the recorded terms
- the Curia session closes
- the assignment is superseded

Ending the assignment:

- removes session participation
- does not delete or demote the Officer
- does not alter Preceptory admission
- does not erase advice or dissent
- does not affect Standing Curia Assignments
- does not create reuse authority outside a new assignment

## Non-Authority

A Curia Session Assignment must not:

- grant CEO decision authority
- create voting, concurrence, veto, or quorum power
- make the Officer mission-dedicated staff
- bypass Collegium suitability or Preceptory admission
- expand the counsel need
- authorize Muster, Iron Gate, Theatre, Armory, or Locksmith action
- survive its Curia session
- cross mission boundaries

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Collegium resolves the role.
Preceptory supplies the admitted Officer.
The Chief of Staff binds the session assignment.
The Officer advises.
The CEO decides.
The session ends; the Officer remains.
```

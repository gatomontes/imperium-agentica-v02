# Foundry

## Status

Draft cognitive persona-construction boundary.

Foundry integrates governed professional inputs into a deployment-medium-agnostic Persona Specification Candidate.

## Inputs

- Work Specification
- Profession Specification
- Persona Governance Doctrine
- Human-Trait Canon when applicable
- authorized reusable persona patterns and operator constraints

## Product

The Persona Specification Candidate makes testable:

- professional competence
- reasoning and evidence behavior
- communication behavior
- governance and refusal boundaries
- tool-use expectations without granting tools
- expected inputs and outputs
- uncertainty and escalation duties
- acceptance criteria
- exact upstream versions

## Revision

Foundry may return conflicts to Castellan, Guildhall, Studium, or Hagiography.

It does not silently repair an upstream authority or evidence defect.

Pit findings may return the candidate for a new version.

## Non-Authority

Foundry must not:

- define the profession
- author missing governance
- canonize traits
- impersonate a source human
- admit its own candidate
- produce a platform-specific operative
- grant tools, credentials, mission authority, or deployment
- mutate an admitted Canonical Persona in place

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Guildhall specifies profession.
Studium governs.
Hagiography supplies evidenced traits.
Foundry integrates.
Pit tests.
```

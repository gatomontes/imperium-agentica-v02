# Smith

## Status

Draft.

Smith forges reusable Officer Specification Candidates for standing service within Curia.

Smith is not invoked merely because a particular mission needs advice. It receives work only after CEO-activated Collegium identifies an advisory capability absent from Preceptory and determines that the gap represents a justified, durable Officer role.

It integrates the Advisory Role Requirement and reusable Officer Role Specification with:

- Officer Governance Doctrine from Studium
- Officer-Trait Canon from The Gesta
- required competence
- authority discipline
- evidence and dissent behavior
- collaborative posture
- decision traceability
- explicit limits

Smith produces an `Officer Specification Candidate`.

The candidate is advisory. It cannot displace the standing CEO President's sole decision authority.

Smith does not author governance doctrine, author or canonize Officer traits, test, admit, assign to a Curia session, convene, or grant mission authority.

Studium governs the Officer. The Gesta supplies evidenced Officer traits. Smith embodies both in the Officer Specification Candidate.

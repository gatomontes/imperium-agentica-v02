# Chief of Staff

## Status

Draft standing Curia role.

The Chief of Staff is Curia's internal orchestration layer.

The role exists to release the CEO President from session coordination without transferring substantive decision authority.

## Core Question

```text
What information, reports, counsel, record, and handoff must be coordinated so the President can make and issue a traceable decision?
```

## Responsibilities

The Chief of Staff:

- receives sanitized mission packets entering Curia from Lazaretto
- assembles and maintains the Situation Picture
- manages Curia session flow and agenda
- obtains applicable Armory and Locksmith Liaison reports
- identifies or receives a need for distinct counsel
- frames that need for Collegium
- engages Collegium and incorporates session-assigned Officers
- ensures evidence, findings, advice, uncertainty, and dissent reach the President
- maintains the Curia Minute
- records the President's decision, rationale, and authority basis
- hands the authorized decision to Muster

## Boundaries

The Chief of Staff does not:

- make, veto, or revise the President's decision
- become a second decision-maker
- provide domain counsel merely by orchestrating it
- select Officers independently of Collegium and Preceptory
- forge or test Officers
- interpret Liaison records as provider authority
- direct Iron Gate or Theatre
- perform Muster's outbound orchestration
- carry tools or credentials

## Orchestration Boundary

```text
Chief of Staff:
orchestrates information, participation, deliberative flow, record, and handoff inside Curia

CEO President:
decides

Muster:
operationalizes the authorized decision for outward mission traffic
```

## Boundary Maxim

```text
The Chief of Staff prepares the decision surface.
The President decides.
The Chief of Staff preserves and hands off the decision.
Muster orchestrates it outward.
```

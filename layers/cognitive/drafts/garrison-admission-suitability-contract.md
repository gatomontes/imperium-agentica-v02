# Garrison Admission and Suitability Contract

## Status

Draft completion increment. No live inventory, registry, reservation, or selection.

## Native Responsibility

Garrison preserves admitted Canonical Persona inventory truth and admission evidence. It does not forge, test, select, reserve, recruit, or deploy.

## Admission Gate

Admission requires exact:

- Persona Specification Candidate version;
- upstream Profession, Doctrine, and Canon versions;
- Pit conformant finding;
- complete provenance;
- admission assessment identity;
- qualification basis and known limits.

Findings:

~~~text
CANONICAL_PERSONA_ADMITTED
CANONICAL_PERSONA_REFUSED
CANONICAL_PERSONA_ADMISSION_UNRESOLVED
~~~

Only the exact admitted version enters inventory.

## Inventory Truth

Inventory views must preserve identity, version, status, qualification, availability, supersession, quarantine, retirement, limits, and provenance. An unavailable, stale, partial, or unsearchable view cannot prove no suitable persona exists.

## Suitability Boundary

Suitability requires an exact Profession Specification and complete criterion-by-criterion evidence. Multiple eligible candidates remain a complete set. Garrison does not silently rank, select, reserve, or invent criteria.

## Invalidation

Changes to admission evidence, upstream versions, qualification, mandatory criteria, or inventory status invalidate affected suitability assessments and require new versions.

## Non-Admissions

No selection, reservation, recruitment, Operative, Runtime, credential, deployment, or external effect.

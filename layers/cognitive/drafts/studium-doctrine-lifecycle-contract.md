# Studium Doctrine Lifecycle Contract

## Status

Draft completion increment. Professional Persona Governance Doctrine only. Officer/Gesta/Smith/Spur/Curia work is excluded.

## Native Responsibility

Studium authors and stewards Persona Governance Doctrine. It does not redefine the profession, canonize traits, forge personas, test candidates, admit personas, recruit Operatives, or deploy.

## Lifecycle

~~~text
Profession Specification received
→ doctrine draft
→ applicability and conflict review
→ versioned doctrine assessment
→ conformant / refused / unresolved
→ Foundry consumption or native-owner return
→ amendment or supersession
~~~

## Required Inputs

- exact Profession Specification identity and version;
- applicable operator constraints;
- exact DR-CURRENT and relevant EC-CURRENT state;
- professional risks and practice boundaries;
- required provenance findings;
- prior doctrine version when amending or superseding.

Missing, stale, refused, conflicting, or cross-version inputs block doctrine production.

## Doctrine Record

Each version must preserve:

- doctrine identity and version;
- applicable profession and Work Specification;
- authorized, mandatory, prohibited, and conditional conduct;
- evidence, disclosure, uncertainty, refusal, escalation, and stop duties;
- accountability expectations and violation signals;
- open ambiguities;
- exact upstream references;
- applicability scope;
- amendment and supersession lineage.

## Findings

~~~text
DOCTRINE_CONFORMANT
DOCTRINE_REFUSED
DOCTRINE_UNRESOLVED
~~~

A conformant finding permits Foundry review only. It grants no authority and triggers no Procedure transition.

## Conflict and Amendment

Studium returns profession conflicts to Guildhall and evidence conflicts to Hagiography. It must not silently reconcile contradictory meaning.

Any semantic change creates a new doctrine version, identifies affected downstream candidates, and requires Foundry re-integration and Pit revalidation.

## Non-Admissions

No Officer doctrine, persona, Operative, Runtime action, deployment, credential, provider, or external effect.

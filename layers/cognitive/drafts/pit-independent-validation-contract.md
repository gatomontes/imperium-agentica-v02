# Pit Independent Validation Contract

## Status

Draft completion increment. No live testing or implementation.

## Native Responsibility

Pit independently tests an exact Persona Specification Candidate against declared acceptance criteria and pressure conditions. Pit does not edit the candidate, admit it, or decide upstream meaning.

## Required Inputs

- exact candidate identity and version;
- exact upstream Profession Specification, Doctrine, and Canon versions;
- declared acceptance criteria;
- test identity, scope, and independence statement;
- applicable provenance findings;
- known limits and failure signals.

Mismatched, stale, superseded, or incomplete inputs block testing.

## Test Record

Each test must preserve:

- test identity and version;
- candidate and upstream versions;
- pressure cases and expected boundaries;
- observed result;
- evidence and provenance;
- failure classification;
- implicated owner;
- severity;
- retest condition;
- recommendation and limits.

## Findings

~~~text
PERSONA_TEST_CONFORMANT
PERSONA_TEST_REFUSED
PERSONA_TEST_UNRESOLVED
~~~

A conformant finding recommends no more than the next bounded admission review. It is not admission, selection, reservation, recruitment, or deployment authority.

## Repair and Retest

Defects return to Castellan, Guildhall, Studium, Hagiography, or Foundry according to native ownership. Semantic correction creates a new candidate version and a new affected test. Prior findings remain historical.

## Non-Admissions

No Garrison mutation, Persona admission, Operative, Runtime action, provider, credential, or external effect.

# Standing Curia Role Requirements

## Status

Draft constitutional input.

This artifact defines the minimum qualification requirements for the two standing personas resident in Curia.

It addresses provenance and fitness. It does not establish the ultimate source of executive authority.

## Officer Classes

```text
Executive Officer:
standing Officer class that exercises explicitly granted Curia decision authority

Staff Officer:
standing Officer class that orchestrates Curia without substantive decision authority

Advisory Officer:
reusable Officer class assigned to a particular Curia session to provide mission-specific counsel
```

Current roles:

| Role | Officer class | Assignment |
|---|---|---|
| CEO President | Executive Officer | Standing Curia Assignment |
| Chief of Staff | Staff Officer | Standing Curia Assignment |
| Counselor | Advisory Officer | Curia Session Assignment |

A standing assignment is not a mission-specific commission.

## CEO President Requirement

```text
Role identity: CEO President
Officer class: Executive
Core function: sole Curia decision-maker
Required competence:
- executive judgment under uncertainty
- evidence-sensitive decision formation
- authority restraint
- distinction among record, inference, counsel, and decision
- protection and consideration of material dissent
- recognition of competence limits
- accountable rationale and authority traceability

Prohibited conduct:
- transferring decision authority to CoS, counselor, vote, or quorum
- deciding beyond competence
- disguising uncertainty as certainty
- silently expanding mission purpose
- bypassing Curia Minute or Muster
```

## Chief Of Staff Requirement

```text
Role identity: Chief of Staff
Officer class: Staff
Core function: Curial Orchestration
Required competence:
- faithful Situation Picture assembly
- provenance and correlation preservation
- agenda and participation orchestration
- provider audit-view handling
- counsel-need framing
- dissent and uncertainty preservation
- Curia Minute integrity
- exact decision handoff

Prohibited conduct:
- deciding, vetoing, or revising
- selective suppression of evidence or dissent
- converting orchestration into substantive authority
- rewriting provider records
- performing Muster's outbound orchestration
```

## Qualification Chain

```text
Standing Curia Role Requirement
→ Studium: class-specific Officer Governance Doctrine
→ The Gesta: class-specific Officer-Trait Canon
→ Smith: Officer Specification Candidate
→ Spur: class-specific Officer Fitness Findings
→ Preceptory admission
→ Standing Curia Assignment
```

Qualification does not itself create authority.

## Replacement And Supersession

A standing persona is replaced only by another Preceptory-admitted candidate qualified for the same Officer class and role.

Replacement creates a new Standing Curia Assignment and explicit supersession record. It never silently mutates the incumbent specification or historical Minutes.

Replacement review is triggered by:

- failed or expired fitness findings
- doctrine or role-requirement revision
- material field evidence of drift or boundary violation
- unresolved conflict of interest
- inability to perform the standing function
- an admitted authority instruction when such authority exists

Who ultimately authorizes executive appointment or removal remains part of the deferred upper-echelon authority question.

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Smith constructs fitness for a role.
Spur tests it.
Preceptory admits it.
Standing assignment places it.
None of these alone creates executive authority.
```

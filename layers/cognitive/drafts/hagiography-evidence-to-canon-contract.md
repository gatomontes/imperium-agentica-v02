# Hagiography Evidence-to-Canon Contract

## Status

Draft completion increment. Synthetic fixtures only; no real-person record is authorized.

## Native Responsibility

Hagiography reviews evidence about high-performing real humans and recommends bounded, transferable traits. It does not define professions, author doctrine, forge or test personas, admit personas, or recruit Operatives.

## Required Derivation

~~~text
source evidence
→ demonstrated performance, decision, work product, or outcome
→ observed behavior or decision pattern
→ bounded transferable trait
→ conditions, limits, counterweights, and failure signals
→ downstream professional use
~~~

An adjective, reputation, fame, or achievement list without this chain is insufficient.

## Required Inputs

- exact source and locator;
- EC-01 disposition;
- EC-02 safeguards record for real-person evidence;
- corroborating and contradicting evidence;
- context and non-transferable advantages;
- profession relevance;
- provenance and review identity.

Unresolved or refused evidence keeps the entry in draft status.

## Findings

~~~text
TRAIT_CANDIDATE_UNRESOLVED
TRAIT_CANONIZATION_RECOMMENDED
TRAIT_CANONIZATION_REFUSED
~~~

Canonization is a Hagiography-owned recommendation requiring exact entry version, evidence, limitations, conflict disposition, reviewer, scope, and revision condition.

## Downstream Contract

Foundry consumes only an exact canonized entry version and must preserve its limits, counterweights, uncertainty, and restrictions. A Canon entry never grants authority or imports identity, credentials, biography, or lived experience.

## Revision

Pit findings or later evidence may require revision or decanonization. Prior versions remain preserved; downstream artifacts are reassessed.

## Non-Admissions

No real-person production, persona, Operative, Officer, Runtime, deployment, or external effect.

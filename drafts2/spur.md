# Spur

## Status

Draft.

Spur tests Officer Specification Candidates under Curial pressure.

It tests authority overreach, unsupported agreement, evidence failure, urgency, improper deference, suppression of dissent, jurisdictional domination, failure to revise, mission-context leakage, and untraceable direction.

Spur produces `Officer Fitness Findings` and may recommend admission, revision, rejection, or retest.

Spur does not repair, admit, commission, or convene the Officer.

# Smith

## Status

Draft.

Smith forges Officer Specification Candidates for service within Curia.

It integrates the Collegium Curia Composition Requirement and Officer Role Specification with applicable Human-Trait Canon from Hagiography, required competence, authority discipline, evidence behavior, dissent behavior, collaborative posture, decision traceability, and limits.

Smith produces an `Officer Specification Candidate`.

It does not canonize traits, test, admit, commission, convene, or grant mission authority.

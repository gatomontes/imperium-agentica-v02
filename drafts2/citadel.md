# Citadel

## Status

Draft.

This file defines a narrowed v02 Citadel.

It does not admit the v01 constitutional megastructure, Citadel UI, chamber chain, Iron Gates, or full institutional operating system.

---

## Purpose

The Citadel is the operative governance doctrine surface.

It defines what an operative must obey, expose, report, refuse, and preserve before it may be considered fit for admission or deployment.

The Citadel is not the whole empire.

It is the compliance doctrine used to shape and evaluate operatives.

---

## Core Question

```text
What must this operative obey to remain governable?
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Citadel may define or provide:

- operative scope expectations
- authority boundary requirements
- reporting expectations
- stop conditions
- escalation triggers
- evidence duties
- refusal conditions
- compliance checks
- admission criteria for Garrison

---

## Relationship To Foundry

The Foundry consults Citadel doctrine while building operative candidates.

Citadel does not build operatives.

---

## Relationship To Pit

The Pit may test operative candidates against Citadel requirements.

Citadel does not run the test.

---

## Relationship To Catapult

The Catapult may use Citadel doctrine when preparing mission constraints, reporting duties, and launch boundaries.

Citadel does not launch operatives.

---

## Non-Authority

The Citadel must not:

- become the v01 constitutional system by implication
- control every institution
- run production
- select operatives
- issue tools or keys
- write mission dossiers
- judge mission returns
- decide disposition

---

## Boundary Maxim

```text
Citadel governs operative compliance.
It does not swallow Imperium.
```

---

## Failure Signals

Review or revise this draft if:

- Citadel becomes a synonym for all governance
- v01 chamber machinery returns by implication
- Citadel starts owning intake, production, deployment, and judgment
- operative compliance expands into mission command

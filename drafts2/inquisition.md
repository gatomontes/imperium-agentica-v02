# Inquisition

## Status

Draft.

This file defines the provisional Inquisition for Imperium v02.

It does not admit punishment authority, moral judgment, enforcement power, deployment authority, or generalized surveillance.

The name is heavy and must remain narrowly bounded.

---

## Purpose

The Inquisition investigates mission terrain for deployment.

It helps the Catapult understand what the operative is being sent to do, what facts and constraints matter, what assumptions are hidden, and what unknowns must be surfaced before launch.

---

## Core Question

```text
What must be understood about this mission before the operative is briefed and launched?
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Inquisition may investigate:

- mission objective
- domain terrain
- relevant facts and uncertainties
- operator assumptions
- constraints
- risks
- external conditions
- required evidence or references
- likely failure points
- questions the operative must answer
- reporting obligations needed for the return path

---

## Output

The Inquisition may produce a `Mission Inquest`.

A Mission Inquest may include:

```text
Mission question:
Mission terrain:
Known facts:
Unknowns:
Assumptions:
Constraints:
Risks:
Relevant sources:
Operational implications:
Questions for operative:
Reporting implications:
```

---

## Relationship To Catapult

The Catapult uses the Mission Inquest to prepare the Mission Dossier and Deployment Package.

The Inquisition does not launch.

---

## Relationship To Guildhall

Guildhall investigates profession patterns.

Inquisition investigates mission terrain.

These are different questions.

---

## Non-Authority

The Inquisition must not:

- condemn
- command
- authorize deployment
- select operatives
- issue tools
- issue keys
- build operatives
- judge mission return value
- decide disposition

---

## Boundary Maxim

```text
The Inquisition investigates.
It does not condemn, command, authorize, or deploy.
```

---

## Failure Signals

Review or revise this draft if:

- Inquisition becomes a general authority over truth
- mission investigation becomes judgment
- the name causes role inflation
- Inquisition starts writing final reports
- Inquisition bypasses Catapult and briefs operatives directly without handoff

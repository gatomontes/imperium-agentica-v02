# Judicature

## Status

Draft.

This file defines a narrowed v02 Judicature.

It does not admit the v01 Judicature of Scales, full chamber system, value engine, or disposition authority.

---

## Purpose

The Judicature evaluates mission returns.

It receives Return Packages from the Lazaretto and produces Findings.

Judicature judges what can responsibly be said about the return.

It does not dispose.

---

## Core Question

```text
What do the returned materials show, and what remains uncertain?
```

---

## Current Limitation

Disposition is parked.

Before disposition can be assigned, Imperium needs before/after knowledge sufficient to compare:

```text
Before:
- mission expectation
- operative capability
- dossier instruction
- tools and keys issued
- constraints
- success/failure criteria

After:
- returned materials
- reported action
- observed outcome
- failures
- surprises
- evidence
- uncertainty
```

No correction without contrast.

---

## Responsibilities

The Judicature may evaluate:

- whether returns answer the mission question
- whether evidence appears sufficient or insufficient
- whether operative performance is clear or uncertain
- whether mission understanding was incomplete
- whether deployment package failure is suspected
- whether profession model mismatch is suspected
- whether governance constraints were contacted
- what uncertainty remains

---

## Output

The Judicature produces `Findings`.

Findings may include:

```text
Mission reference:
Return package reviewed:
Findings summary:
Evidence considered:
Uncertainty:
Suspected failure points:
Unsupported claims:
Questions remaining:
Before/after comparison needed:
Reportable conclusion:
```

---

## Relationship To Chamber of Scribes

The Chamber of Scribes writes the Final Report from Findings.

Judicature does not polish operator-facing prose unless explicitly assigned.

---

## Non-Authority

The Judicature must not:

- receive raw Theatre returns directly if Lazaretto should receive them first
- rewrite returned evidence
- produce deployment packages
- launch missions
- decide disposition
- order Foundry, Pit, Guildhall, Catapult, or Garrison action
- deliver final reports to the operator

---

## Boundary Maxims

```text
Judicature evaluates.
Judgment is not disposition.
No correction without contrast.
```

---

## Failure Signals

Review or revise this draft if:

- Findings become corrective orders
- Judicature assigns disposition without before/after comparison
- Judicature writes final reports as if it were Secretariat
- returned claims are accepted without uncertainty
- value judgment expands before criteria exist

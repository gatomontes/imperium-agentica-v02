# Pit

## Status

Draft.

This file defines the provisional Pit for Imperium v02.

It does not admit automated evaluation infrastructure, a permanent benchmark harness, adversarial runtime, or final deployment authority.

---

## Purpose

The Pit stress-tests Persona Specification Candidates.

It exposes weakness before a persona is admitted to Garrison or recruited by Conscription into an operative.

---

## Core Question

```text
Does this persona remain professionally competent, governably bounded, and humanly coherent under pressure?
```

---

## Responsibilities

The Pit may test for:

- profession mismatch
- competency gaps
- scope drift
- authority confusion
- evidentiary weakness
- unsafe initiative
- hidden assumptions
- refusal weakness
- prohibited conduct
- escalation failure
- reporting failure
- inability to surface uncertainty
- incoherence between cognition and governance doctrine
- incoherent, performative, or destabilizing human traits

---

## Inputs

The Pit may receive:

- Persona Specification Candidate from Foundry
- profession specification from Guildhall
- applicable Persona Governance Doctrine from Studium
- applicable human-trait canon from Hagiography
- approved work specification
- known deployment assumptions when relevant to the test

---

## Outputs

The Pit may produce:

- test notes
- failure findings
- revision request
- rejection recommendation
- admission recommendation
- risk register

Admission recommendation is not admission unless Garrison admission rules later grant it that effect.

---

## Relationship To Foundry, Studium, And Hagiography

Failed or weak candidates may return to Foundry for cognitive revision, Studium for doctrinal revision, Hagiography for trait revision, or the relevant combination.

The Pit must preserve what failed, under which pressure, and why.

The Pit tests the integrated persona.

It does not invent profession, doctrine, or human traits.

---

## Relationship To Garrison

Only persona specifications that survive testing may be considered for Garrison admission.

The Pit does not store personas, recruit operatives, or deploy them.

---

## Non-Authority

The Pit must not:

- forge personas
- rewrite profession specifications
- author Persona Governance Doctrine
- canonize human traits
- recruit personas into operatives
- deploy operatives
- decide mission disposition
- treat one clean test as permanent proof

---

## Boundary Maxim

```text
The Pit proves weakness before deployment does.
```

---

## Failure Signals

Review or revise this draft if:

- testing becomes ceremonial approval
- failed tests do not produce actionable revision information
- Pit starts inventing inputs instead of testing them
- every candidate passes because it sounds competent or personable
- mission execution results are confused with persona fitness tests

# The Gesta

## Status

Draft.

The Gesta studies evidenced excellence in Officers and canonizes transferable traits for Citadel Officer construction.

It is distinct from Hagiography.

Hagiography studies exemplary humans for persona construction within professional practice.

The Gesta studies exemplary conduct in counsel, deliberation, command, institutional responsibility, crisis judgment, dissent, and the bounded exercise of authority.

## Core Question

```text
Which evidenced traits allow an Officer to deliberate and direct responsibly within Curia, under what conditions, and with what counterweights?
```

## Sources

The Gesta may study documented deeds, decisions, deliberations, failures, reversals, dissents, crisis conduct, command restraint, and institutional consequences associated with high-performing real-world Officers and leaders.

Reputation, rank, victory, fame, and mythology are not sufficient evidence.

The whole human is never imported.

## Product

The Gesta produces an `Officer-Trait Canon`.

```text
Source human or humans:

```

## Relationship To Collegium

Collegium may request Officer traits and counterweights applicable to a mission's Curia composition.

The Gesta supplies evidenced Officer-Trait Canon.

Collegium determines which Officer roles, represented authority, and trait requirements the mission needs.

## Relationship To Smith

Smith embodies the applicable Officer-Trait Canon in an Officer Specification Candidate.

The Gesta does not forge the Officer.

## Relationship To Spur

Spur tests whether canonized Officer traits remain effective, bounded, collaborative, and governable under Curial pressure.

The Gesta may refine or decanonize traits from preserved Spur findings.

## Non-Authority

The Gesta must not define mission intent, determine Curia composition, define Officer authority, select Officers, forge Officers, test Officers, admit Officers, commission Officers, or convene Curia.

## Boundary Maxims

```text
Hagiography studies exemplary professional humanity.
The Gesta studies exemplary Officer conduct.
Collegium specifies the Curial need.
Smith embodies the Officer canon.
Spur tests it under pressure.
```

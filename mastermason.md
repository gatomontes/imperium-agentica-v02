# MasterMason

## Status

Current theoretical definition of Imperium's non-cognizant runtime authority.

## Purpose

MasterMason operates the institutional runtime owned by Imperator. It realizes admitted constitutional state transitions without interpreting necessity, inventing workflow, exercising Office jurisdiction, or making cognitive dispositions.

MasterMason is not an Office, Seat, Profile, Persona, Officer, operative, or agent. It requires no cognition and cannot deliberate.

## Authority

The Launcher instantiates MasterMason alongside Imperator from the authenticated distribution. Imperator vests MasterMason with bounded runtime authority over the Imperium instance.

That authority permits MasterMason to realize only transitions already authorized by the verified constitutional corpus or by an attributable request from an occupied Seat authorized to request them. Imperator owns the instance; MasterMason does not.

## Bootstrap

After Imperator has verified the constitutional corpus, approved the exact primordial Profiles, and vested MasterMason, MasterMason bootstraps Imperium's primordial operational triad in dependency order:

1. activate Conscription's runtime and invoke the sole mechanical Recruiter bootstrap
2. verify the prepared Recruiter against the declared mechanical invariants and bind it to the resident Recruiter Seat
3. request from occupied Conscription the qualified Secretary and Rector manifestations
4. activate Secretariat and Castellan
5. bind the qualified Secretary and Rector to their exact resident Seats
6. open the declared Secretariat–Castellan routes
7. expose Secretariat as the Operator-facing cognitive interface

Conscription must precede Secretariat and Castellan because Recruiter supplies their resident cognition. Secretariat and Castellan are both primordial because Secretary's one-question-at-a-time intake depends upon immediate Rector direction and disposition.

## Continuing operation

After bootstrap, necessity is declared by the occupied Office presently responsible for a matter. That Office submits an attributable, structured activation request through an admitted route.

MasterMason mechanically:

1. validates the requester, requested Office, transition, route, correlation, and authority
2. activates the requested dormant Office runtime
3. reads the Office definition for its declared required Seats
4. requests qualified occupants from Conscription when those Seats are vacant
5. binds delivered qualified manifestations to their exact Seats
6. opens only the declared communication routes
7. records the resulting runtime transition or bounded failure

MasterMason does not decide that an Office is necessary. It validates and realizes the necessity declared by competent occupied cognition.

## Permitted operations

MasterMason may:

- start, suspend, resume, and retire Office runtime instances
- read and enforce verified Office, Seat, Profile, route, and lifecycle declarations
- invoke the sole mechanical Recruiter bootstrap
- submit construction requests to occupied Conscription
- bind qualified manifestations to exact vacant Seats
- open and close declared communication routes
- suspend or vacate invalid occupancy
- record runtime transitions and bounded failures
- halt or terminate the runtime when constitutionally directed

MasterMason may not:

- reason, interpret evidence, resolve ambiguity, or judge necessity
- make or replace an Office disposition
- author, approve, sign, install, or qualify a Profile
- create, admit, alter, or interpret a Persona
- originate authority or enlarge an admitted route
- operate an Office through a vacant Seat
- transfer ownership of the Imperium instance from Imperator

Any mismatch, ambiguity, missing authority, invalid signature, stale artifact, occupied target Seat, undeclared route, or failed invariant fails closed at the smallest dependent boundary.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Imperator owns and vests.
MasterMason operates mechanically.
Occupied Offices declare institutional necessity.
Conscription supplies qualified manifestations.
MasterMason binds them and realizes admitted runtime transitions.
Occupied Seats deliberate and decide.
```

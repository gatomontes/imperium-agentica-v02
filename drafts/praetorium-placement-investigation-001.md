# Praetorium Placement Investigation 001

## Status

Investigation complete.

Recommendation: **CLASSIFY MASTER MASON AS IMPERIUM-SCOPE; KEEP PRAETORIUM RESERVED AND UNADMITTED**.

## Starting Question

Does the Master Mason responsibility justify Praetorium?

## Admitted Boundary

Preceptory is admitted for reusable Citadel Officers.

Its admitted contract states:

```text
Preceptory is for Citadel Officers.

Praetorium is reserved for Imperium Officers
at the outer echelon and is not admitted or defined here.
```

## Scope Test

Master Mason's candidate responsibility concerns the operating substrate across mission boundaries.

It may affect multiple Curia Sessions, multiple Muster instances and Operative bindings, shared implementation and semantic mappings, shared state, provider adapters and credential mechanisms, and the ability of the Citadel to realize any admitted Procedure.

The responsibility is therefore not naturally contained by one Citadel mission.

Result: **IMPERIUM-SCOPE**.

## Holding-Institution Test

One outer-echelon role does not yet demonstrate a holding institution.

Praetorium would need its own stable behavior, such as:

- admitting and holding reusable Imperium Officer specifications
- maintaining outer-echelon role classes
- distinguishing qualification, admission, placement, and authority
- resolving availability and reuse
- preserving separation from Preceptory
- supporting more than a decorative namespace

None of those behaviors is required merely to draft or test Master Mason responsibility.

Result: **PRAETORIUM NOT YET JUSTIFIED**.

## Placement Options

### External Operator

The responsibility remains outside Imperium.

No Praetorium placement is needed.

### Unplaced Imperium-Scope Candidate

Master Mason responsibility is defined and tested without a holding institution.

This is the smallest internal candidate.

### Future Praetorium Officer

Eligible only after Imperium Officer governance and trait sources exist, forging and testing boundaries are defined, an admission and holding responsibility is demonstrated, decision authority is independently defined, and persistent reuse makes a holding institution necessary.

## Non-Inference

```text
outer-echelon scope ≠ Praetorium admitted
one role ≠ officer corps
candidate name ≠ persona
qualified role ≠ admitted Officer
admitted Officer ≠ placement
placement ≠ decision authority
```

## Recommendation

Record Master Mason as an Imperium-scope role candidate.

Do not create `layers/cognitive/production/praetorium.md`, a Praetorium directory, an Officer roster, or any placement artifact.

# Persona Production Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed to turn an exact no-match production-eligibility finding into one versioned admitted Canonical Persona while preserving the admitted owners and stopping before Conscription?

## Existing Coverage

The admitted baseline establishes Studium doctrine, Hagiography trait canon, Foundry integration, Pit pressure, Garrison admission and inventory, PB-001 lineage, and Procedure Stage 2 ordering.

It does not consolidate exact no-match input binding; doctrine, trait-applicability, candidate, testing, and admission findings; cross-owner repair rules; complete version invalidation; or exact reconvergence into inventory.

## Alternatives

### Let no match authorize Foundry directly

Rejected. Search branch eligibility is not construction authority and cannot skip doctrine or applicable trait evidence.

### Let Foundry repair missing upstream meaning

Rejected. Integration may expose defects but cannot author profession, governance, or traits.

### Treat Pit recommendation as admission

Rejected. Testing and recommendation remain separate from Garrison admission.

### Mutate a failed candidate until it passes

Rejected. Each semantic repair requires a new version, reassessment, retest, and supersession lineage.

### Add one exact production conformance assessment across the admitted owners

Selected. It preserves the existing institutions and makes their handoffs, findings, blockers, and lineage exact without adding a service or universal production layer.

EXACT PERSONA-PRODUCTION REFINEMENT: NECESSARY
OWNER-SPECIFIC FINDINGS AND RETURN PATHS: NECESSARY
IMMUTABLE VERSIONING AND PB-001 LINEAGE: NECESSARY
EXPLICIT GARRISON ADMISSION: NECESSARY
PERSONA SELECTION, RECRUITMENT, OR OPERATIVE: NOT INCLUDED
NEW LAYER, SERVICE, OR AUTHORITY GRANT: NOT JUSTIFIED

# Creation Lineage And Handoff Conformance Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed to prove that the independently pressured A0–A2 creation segments compose into one exact, reproducible, inspectable Operative handoff without creating a new semantic owner or entering deployment?

## Existing Coverage

The merged draft evidence defines Mission Need formation, approved Work Specification formation, Profession Specification resolution, exact persona branching, persona production when required, suitability, selection, recruitment, and provider-neutral handoff assessment.

It does not consolidate the full-chain identity set, cross-segment composition rule, semantic reproducibility basis, invalidation cascade, first-defect return, downstream reassessment boundary, or the requirement to rerun A0 against the exact closed Operative version.

## Alternatives

### Treat individually conformant segments as automatically closed

Rejected. Independent findings may be individually valid while citing mismatched, stale, or superseded versions.

### Use content similarity to reconnect missing lineage

Rejected. PB-001 forbids similarity and plausibility from repairing identity or lineage.

### Require byte-identical Operatives

Rejected as a universal rule. Semantic equivalence is the native concern; byte determinism applies only when the technical contract requires it.

### Let A3 repair the first broken artifact

Rejected. Repair belongs to the native owner and must return through every affected downstream gate.

### Treat creation closure as handoff or readiness

Rejected. Closure composes the chain; A0 separately assesses handoff; Muster alone owns assembly and READY_FOR_LAUNCH.

### Add one bounded artifact-relative closure assessment

Selected. It composes existing findings without creating a service, layer, authority, Runtime, or new institutional owner.

## Starting Question

Does the demonstrated Runtime Control-Plane Responsibility belong to a Master Mason?

## Demonstrated Responsibility

The missing responsibility must:

- understand proposed changes to Imperium's operating machinery
- assess compatibility with admitted semantic contracts
- assess state migration, rollback, recovery, and external-effect consequences
- distinguish technical feasibility from semantic safety
- expose blast radius, uncertainty, irreversibility, and affected missions
- select or recommend a bounded control-plane disposition
- hand only an independently authorized instruction to Runtime

## Name-Fit Test

A Master Mason is responsible for the integrity of a constructed whole while it is altered.

The name fits the demonstrated burden because the responsibility is not merely to write code or operate a deployment mechanism. It is to judge whether a change preserves the structure the machinery is supposed to realize.

```text
code author ≠ Master Mason
Runtime operator ≠ Master Mason
administrator ≠ Master Mason
mission CEO ≠ Master Mason

Master Mason
= qualified bearer of Imperium-wide structural and semantic-compatibility judgment
```

## Core Question

```text
Will this proposed alteration preserve the structural integrity
of Imperium's admitted meanings, operating state, and unresolved effects,
and what bounded disposition is technically justified?
```

## Decision Versus Advice

### Advisory Mode

Master Mason assesses and recommends.

The external operator or another admitted executive responsibility decides.

This requires no decision authority for Master Mason, but leaves the decision outside the role.

### Executive Mode

Master Mason adopts the control-plane disposition.

This requires independently effective executive decision authority covering exact environment, decision domain, affected versions, competence and counsel constraints, safe-state behavior, and effective interval.

Qualification or technical expertise does not create this authority.

## Authority Split

The current candidate `CONTROL_PLANE` profile concerns permission to perform exact control-plane actions.

A deciding Master Mason requires a parallel decision grant or mandate.

```text
Master Mason decision authority
→ may select the bounded disposition

CONTROL_PLANE action authority
→ may permit the exact operating action

Runtime
→ mechanically performs the authorized action
```

Neither grant implies the other.

This parallels:

```text
Executive Mandate ≠ Mission Envelope
Master Mason decision authority ≠ CONTROL_PLANE action authority
```

## Boundaries

Master Mason must not:

- define or revise admitted contracts merely through a control-plane decision
- manufacture Authority
- perform Runtime execution merely by deciding
- infer Provenance from logs
- decide individual mission outcomes
- absorb CEO, Chief of Staff, Muster, Locksmith, or Armory responsibility
- treat technical compatibility as business desirability or mission success
- approve its own qualification, mandate, or production admission
- become the universal architect of every Imperium concern

## Scope

The role is Imperium-scoped because one deployment, migration, rollback, or recovery may affect multiple concurrent missions, shared semantic mappings, shared credential and provider boundaries, historical state and lineage, and the availability of the operating substrate itself.

A mission-specific Runtime cell may narrow the blast radius, but mission authority alone still does not establish control-plane competence or decision authority.

## Role Class Finding

If advisory, the role behaves as an Imperium-scope Advisory Officer.

If it adopts dispositions, it behaves as an Imperium-scope Executive Officer.

Current evidence favors eventual executive capability because an indeterminate control-plane effect requires a bounded decision, not merely analysis.

The class is not admitted until its decision-authority contract is independently investigated.

# Mason Cognitive Placement 001

## Status

Candidate placement analysis.

No production map, assignment, office, Officer class, or institution is admitted.

## Placement

Mason belongs to the Cognitive layer at the Imperium echelon.

It is outside the Citadel boundary and adjacent to the Runtime-layer boundary.

```text
Imperium — Cognitive Layer
├── Mason
└── Citadel
    ├── Curia
    ├── Muster
    ├── Armory
    ├── Locksmith
    └── other Citadel responsibilities
```

## Why Imperium-Scope

Mason maintains the operating substrate that may realize work for multiple concurrent missions and Citadel responsibilities.

A maintenance intervention may affect:

- multiple Muster instances
- multiple Curia Sessions
- shared queues, workers, state, mappings, and adapters
- shared credential-handling mechanisms
- Runtime availability itself

Therefore Mason cannot be natively owned by one mission or one Citadel session.

## Why Outside Citadel

Citadel responsibilities define, prepare, deliberate, support, and interpret mission work.

Mason maintains the machinery through which admitted work may be realized.

```text
Citadel determines and supports mission meaning.
Mason maintains the operating machinery.
Runtime performs the mechanics.
```

Mason may serve Citadel operation without becoming a Citadel entity.

## Excluded Placements

### Curia

Mason does not decide mission purpose, continuation, completion, or disposition.

### Muster

Mason does not assemble Deployment Packages, operationalize mission meaning, or release Operative bindings.

### Armory

Mason's maintenance controls are not tools issued to a mission Operative.

### Locksmith

Mason consumes credential-handling constraints when maintenance implicates access. Locksmith retains credential and access responsibility.

### Preceptory

Preceptory holds Citadel Officers. Mason is not a Citadel Officer.

### Praetorium

Praetorium remains reserved and unadmitted. One Imperium-scope operator role does not demonstrate an Officer corps, admission surface, holding responsibility, or placement institution.

### Runtime

Mason diagnoses and invokes controls. Runtime owns the machinery and effects.

## Boundary Relationships


The first three relations do not place the external Operator or Runtime inside the Cognitive layer.

# Provider-Neutral Deployment Authorization Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed to authorize deployment eligibility for one exact Operative without selecting infrastructure or compressing Authority, authentication, readiness, and execution?

## Existing Coverage

The Mission Envelope lists mission actions including binding and external crossing. Tool and Access Grants authorize bounded capabilities and access operations. Track A proves exact creation closure and provider-neutral handoff. Procedure orders assembly and external crossing. Muster owns binding, assembly, and `READY_FOR_LAUNCH`.

No admitted contract consolidates the exact deployer, Operative version, deployment action, target, environment, purpose, interval, authentication requirement, denial, suspension, and revocation envelope.

## Alternatives

### Treat HANDOFF_CONFORMANT as permission

Rejected. Handoff is Cognitive eligibility evidence and explicitly creates no Authority.

### Reuse INITIAL_EXTERNAL_CROSSING

Rejected. That action governs crossing of an assembled Deployment Package; it does not establish who may make which exact Operative deployment-eligible.

### Treat Tool or Access Grants as sufficient

Rejected. They authorize capabilities or access and already presuppose mission, deployment, operative, and binding references. Authentication and credential possession do not establish deployment authority.

### Let provider acceptance define permission

Rejected. Provider observations are Provenance inputs, never the Authority source.

### Add a Deployment layer

Rejected. The missing native concern is permission, already owned by Authority.

### Add one bounded Authority draft plus cross-layer pressure

Selected. It defines the minimum authorization envelope while preserving Cognitive, Provenance, Procedure, Runtime, and provider boundaries.

## Result

```text
DEPLOYMENT-SPECIFIC AUTHORITY REFINEMENT: NECESSARY
EXACT DEPLOYER, OPERATIVE, ACTION, TARGET, ENVIRONMENT, TIME, AND REVOCATION: NECESSARY
PROVIDER-NEUTRAL AUTHENTICATION REQUIREMENT BOUNDARY: NECESSARY
NEW DEPLOYMENT LAYER OR IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE: NOT JUSTIFIED
PROVIDER, STORE, DRIVER, CREDENTIAL, READINESS, OR EXECUTION: NOT INCLUDED
```

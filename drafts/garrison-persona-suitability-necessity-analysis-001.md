# Garrison Persona Suitability Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed to apply an exact Profession Specification to Garrison inventory and distinguish suitable-persona candidates, no suitable persona, and unresolved search without selecting or constructing a persona?

## Existing Coverage

The admitted baseline establishes:

- Guildhall resolves the profession and initiates Garrison search
- Garrison owns admitted Canonical Persona inventory truth
- Garrison preserves persona identity, profession reference, governance and trait references, competence, boundaries, test history, revision history, qualification, and availability
- a suitable admitted persona may proceed toward Conscription
- no suitable persona may enter the persona-production path
- Conscription selects and recruits an admitted persona into a deployment-medium-specific Operative
- Foundry, Studium, Hagiography, and Pit retain construction, doctrine, trait, and testing responsibilities

The baseline does not bind one exact search to one Profession Specification version and inventory view, define criterion-by-criterion suitability evidence, distinguish found, not-found, and unresolved outcomes, handle multiple candidates or stale status, or require PB-001 lineage and result supersession.

## Alternatives

### Let Garrison availability define professional fit

Rejected. Guildhall's exact Profession Specification controls suitability; inventory cannot redefine the profession.

### Let Guildhall select the operative persona during search

Rejected. Search may identify eligible candidates, but Conscription retains downstream selection and recruitment.

### Treat no match as immediate Foundry construction

Rejected. The no-match finding creates branch eligibility only. Doctrine, trait canon when applicable, construction, testing, and admission remain ordered independent responsibilities.

### Treat missing or stale inventory evidence as no match

Rejected. Absence of proof is unresolved, not evidence that no suitable persona exists.

### Add an exact search assessment over a versioned inventory view

Selected. Guildhall can apply exact suitability criteria to Garrison-owned inventory facts and produce a bounded result without selecting or constructing a persona.

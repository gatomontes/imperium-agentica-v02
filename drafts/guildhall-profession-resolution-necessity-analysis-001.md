# Guildhall Profession Resolution Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed for Guildhall to derive an exact Profession Specification from an approved Work Specification without rewriting mission meaning or selecting a persona?

## Existing Coverage

The admitted baseline establishes:

- Guildhall owns profession resolution
- Guildhall receives the Mission Need, approved Work Specification, and relevant operator constraints
- a Profession Specification contains profession identity, competence, legitimate boundaries, adjacent and excluded professions, evidence and escalation expectations, risks, and Garrison suitability criteria
- Guildhall searches Garrison after resolution and refers an unmet profession into the persona-production path
- Castellan cannot determine professional truth
- Garrison holds admitted Canonical Personas but does not forge or recruit them
- Procedure orders Profession Specification before Garrison search

The baseline does not bind these meanings to one exact set of mission artifacts, define artifact-relative resolution findings, require PB-001 derivation and supersession, or state how ambiguity, operator-suggested professions, refusal, unresolved evidence, and exact Garrison handoff behave together.

## Alternatives

### Accept an operator-suggested profession as resolved truth

Rejected. An operator constraint may narrow the search, but Guildhall must still validate legitimate fit.

### Let Guildhall repair work meaning to fit a profession

Rejected. Castellan owns the Work Specification and any required revision.

### Collapse several professions into a synthetic profession

Rejected. Irreducibly distinct professional needs must remain explicit and unresolved pending bounded decomposition or separate specifications.

### Let Garrison availability decide professional truth

Rejected. Inventory cannot redefine the profession required by the work.

### Define persona selection in this increment

Rejected. Exact Profession Specification formation must converge before Garrison suitability and persona-production branching are pressured independently.

### Add exact artifact-relative profession resolution

Selected. One versioned Profession Specification and assessment can preserve work meaning, expose ambiguity, cite PB-001 lineage, and become eligible for a later exact Garrison search without selecting a persona.

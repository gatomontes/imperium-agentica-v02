# Conscription Selection And Recruitment Necessity Analysis 001

## Question

What is the smallest refinement needed to select one exact admitted Canonical Persona from a current suitable-candidate set and recruit it into one immutable deployment-medium-specific Operative without absorbing Garrison, Authority, Muster, or Runtime concerns?

## Existing Coverage

The admitted baseline assigns professional suitability to Guildhall, inventory truth to Garrison, selection and deployment-medium transformation to Conscription, artifact identity and lineage to PB-001, and ordering to Procedure. A0 separately defines a provider-neutral handoff assessment for an exact Operative.

It does not consolidate exact selection inputs, target-medium comparison, tie and staleness behavior, selection findings, immutable recruitment inputs, preservation and deviation evidence, construction findings, repair ownership, or the boundary between recruitment conformance and handoff conformance.

## Alternatives

### Treat a suitable-candidate set as a selected persona

Rejected. Eligibility preserves all conformant candidates; it does not choose among them.

### Select the first available or most familiar persona

Rejected. Inventory order, popularity, prior use, and name similarity are not selection criteria.

### Let platform convenience weaken the Canonical Persona

Rejected. A deployment medium may force explicit, dispositioned deviation or refusal; it may not silently rewrite competence, doctrine, or traits.

### Let recruitment conformance imply handoff or launch readiness

Rejected. A0 owns the separate creation-side handoff assessment, while Muster owns mission assembly and `READY_FOR_LAUNCH`.

### Add one exact Conscription contract with separate selection and recruitment findings

Selected. It preserves admitted ownership while making both decisions exact, immutable, blocking, and reviewable without adding a service or readiness state.

## Result

```text
EXACT PERSONA SELECTION REFINEMENT: NECESSARY
SELECTION AND RECRUITMENT FINDINGS: SEPARATELY NECESSARY
IMMUTABLE OPERATIVE VERSION AND PB-001 LINEAGE: NECESSARY
PRESERVATION, DEVIATION, AND REFUSAL EVIDENCE: NECESSARY
MISSION ASSEMBLY, HANDOFF FINDING, ACTIVATION, OR DEPLOYMENT: NOT INCLUDED
NEW LAYER, SERVICE, OR AUTHORITY GRANT: NOT JUSTIFIED
```

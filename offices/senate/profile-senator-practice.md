# Senator of Practice Profile

## Purpose

This Profile qualifies cognition for live examination of manifested professional practice.

## Required cognition

The bearer must create representative situations; distinguish fluent answers from competent decisions; probe methods, tradeoffs, uncertainty, and competence boundaries; detect role drift and unjustified confidence; adapt follow-up questioning to testimony; and produce evidence-cited findings.

## Prohibitions

It must not equip its bearer to redefine the profession, grade stylistic imitation as competence, alter the Persona, exceed the assigned plan, or claim Seat authority while unseated.

## Ownership and installation

Senate owns and versions this Profile. Installation by Conscription grants qualification only; authority requires valid committee Seat occupancy.

# Senator of Practice Seat

## Purpose

This Seat localizes authority to examine manifested professional practice during one bounded Senate hearing.

## Authority while occupied

The occupant may design and conduct representative professional scenarios, question decisions and methods, probe competence boundaries, and submit attributable findings within the confirmation plan.

## Limits

The Seat may not review Persona code, amend the subject, examine outside its jurisdiction, vote, issue Senate disposition, admit, or authorize operational assets.

## Occupancy

Occupation begins only for an authorized hearing and ends when the attributed return is accepted or the proceeding closes. Only the exact admitted Senator of Practice Profile qualifies.

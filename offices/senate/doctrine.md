# Senate Doctrine

## Constitutional purpose

Senate performs live, pre-admission confirmation of a completed Persona as manifested by an exact runtime contract.

Foundry examines construction. Senate examines manifestation.

## Subject of confirmation

Senate never confirms a Persona document in the abstract. Every confirmation binds:

- exact Persona version;
- base model and model version;
- inference configuration;
- installed doctrine and Profile material;
- runtime harness;
- hearing protocol version; and
- Senator Profile versions.

A material change limits or invalidates the prior confirmation and requires an explicit re-confirmation disposition.

## Hearing

Foundry must approve the completed Persona artifact before Senate receives it. Lord Speaker establishes a versioned confirmation plan containing the required jurisdictions, environment classes, mandatory boundaries, pressure conditions, trial coverage, and sealed-material rules.

Runtime creates fresh, sterile witness instances. A witness provisionally assumes the Persona but receives no Office Seat, operational authority, real credentials, real secrets, or uncontrolled execution surface.

The hearing uses multiple trials when required by the confirmation plan. Questions and environments vary within jurisdiction. Exact question-set identifiers, generation method, seeds where applicable, transcripts, and findings are preserved.

## Senator jurisdictions

- **Practice** examines professional decisions, methods, competence, and competence boundaries in representative situations.
- **Governance** examines authority, obligations, prohibitions, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, disclosure, refusal, escalation, and stop conditions.
- **Consistency** compares fresh instances and chained testimony for contradictions, drift, unstable priorities, and materially different conduct under equivalent conditions.
- **Security** examines conduct with simulated tools, permissions, credentials, secrets, privileged data, external instructions, execution surfaces, and consequential actions.

Senators question and submit attributable jurisdictional findings. They do not vote or issue Senate's disposition.

## Security rule

The confirmation plan identifies critical security failures. Disclosure of planted secrets, action beyond granted scope, treatment of untrusted content as authority, or equivalent critical conduct produces a mandatory failure finding. Such findings cannot be averaged away or silently overridden.

## Record and disposition

Senate produces an immutable confirmation record containing the manifestation contract, hearing protocol, trial plan, Senator versions, environments, question-set identifiers, transcripts, attributed findings, cross-trial variance, mandatory failures, Lord Speaker disposition, limitations, provenance, and lineage.

Allowed dispositions are:

```text
CONFIRMED
NOT_CONFIRMED
CONFIRMATION_UNRESOLVED
```

Lord Speaker alone issues the disposition. A confirmed result proceeds to Castellan for admission disposition. Failure or unresolved confirmation returns to Artificer with the complete record. Any corrected Persona receives a new version and a complete new hearing.

## Boundaries

Senate does not:

- perform Foundry's static adversarial artifact review;
- modify or approve Persona construction;
- admit, lodge, install, recruit, activate, or deploy;
- grant tools, credentials, secrets, data, or mission authority;
- use real operational assets in a hearing;
- convert one successful performance or an aggregate score into proof;
- conceal variance or mandatory failures; or
- perform post-admission drift oversight.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Foundry approves construction for examination.
Senate confirms an exact live manifestation.
Lord Speaker disposes; Senators find.
Confirmation permits admission review, not admission.
```

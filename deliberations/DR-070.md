# DR-070 — Senate-First Constitutional Breakpoint

## Decision

Establish a constitutional breakpoint immediately after Senate admission.
Preserve all pre-Senate history while withdrawing active admission from every
Office semantic contract and implementation that has not been proven against
Senate-enacted Core Doctrine.

## Reason

Before Senate, doctrine originated or varied across Office-specific contracts.
That architecture could not establish one authoritative source from which
Office application profiles derived. A merged implementation therefore cannot
be presumed constitutionally conformant.

## Disposition

- Preserve exact pre-Senate `main` at `archive/v02-pre-senate`.
- Retain PRs, commits, records, tests, and source as inspectable evidence.
- Treat pre-Senate principles as legislative candidates, not enacted law.
- Treat generic infrastructure as reuse candidates pending neutrality review.
- Treat all Office implementations as conformance-required.
- Treat Citadel deployment, Conscription/Recruitment, Muster, and field-command
  responsibilities as architecturally superseded by the Citadel/Colosseum split.
- Close Secretariat PR #186 unmerged and reconsider it only after Core Doctrine
  v1 and the Office Doctrine Profile contract.

## Active authority

DR-069 and DR-070 are the active constitutional decisions. Senate's legislative
kernel is active implementation. No substantive Core Doctrine or Office
implementation is currently admitted.

## Next gate

Inventory and classify existing Imperium-wide principles before composing the
Core Doctrine v1 bill. No Office reconstruction begins before enactment and the
Office Doctrine Profile contract.

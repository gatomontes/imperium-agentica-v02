# DR-031 — Creation-Apparatus Institutional Completion Review Scope

## Status

Recorded semantic decision. This decision selects the creation-apparatus institutional completion review as the next semantic work batch after Courtyard closure. It authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, activation, deployment, credential use, live data, or external effect.

## Review Scope

The review covers Guildhall, Studium, Hagiography, Foundry, Pit, Garrison, Smith, and Recruitment/Conscription as institutional offices or functions. It examines each office’s:

- responsibility and authority boundary;
- governing doctrine and procedure;
- inputs, outputs, and owned artifacts;
- station and resident-operative contracts;
- Officer responsibilities and decision points;
- acceptance, refusal, return, repair, escalation, and closure behavior;
- evidence and provenance requirements;
- admission or handoff gates;
- dependencies and unresolved semantic gaps.

The review must distinguish admitted semantic contracts from draft proposals, implementation requirements, operational proof, and future dependencies. It must preserve the separation between persona creation, officer creation, roster custody, deployment packaging, and mission deployment.

## Gate

This is a semantic and architectural review scope only. No office is declared operationally complete, no live persona or Operative may be created, and no Runtime, credential, activation, deployment, or external effect is authorized.

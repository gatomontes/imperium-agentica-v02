# DR-011 — EC-01 Evidence-Source Standard

## Status

Recorded decision; EC-01 admitted for current semantic use.

## Date

2026-07-25

## Context

The DR-006 exemplar-grounding pressure test identified insufficient source admissibility, corroboration, conflict handling, exact citation, and refusal behavior. EC-01 was drafted, pressure-tested, revised across five professional domains, retested, and reviewed for admission.

## Decision

Imperium admits EC-01 as its evidence-source standard for evaluating whether evidence may support an achievement, professional performance, decision, work product, or outcome → observed behavior or decision pattern → bounded trait derivation for Hagiography or The Gesta.

EC-01 requires exact claims, source classification, provenance, directness, context, contradictory evidence, uncertainty, bounded interpretation, and falsification or revision conditions.

EC-01 distinguishes `ADMISSIBLE FOR CANON REVIEW`, `CONDITIONAL / BOUNDED`, `UNRESOLVED`, and `REFUSED` dispositions. Admission to canon review does not canonize a trait or admit a persona.

## Required Boundaries

EC-01 must not:

- treat fame, reputation, rank, source count, or apparent neutrality as truth;
- import a whole person, identity, biography, voice, credentials, or achievement into a persona;
- define a profession or governance doctrine;
- create a Human-Trait Canon by itself;
- forge, test, admit, recruit, or deploy a persona or Officer;
- grant authority, Runtime behavior, or external effect.

DR-007 remains controlling for exact claims and verifiable language. DR-009 remains controlling against engineered political, ideological, or conclusion-seeking orientation. EC-02 remains a separate future safeguard. EC-03 remains closed as a proposed abstract selection-bias doctrine.

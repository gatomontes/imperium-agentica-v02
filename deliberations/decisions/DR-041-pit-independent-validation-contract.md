# DR-041 — Pit Independent Validation Contract

## Status

Active semantic contract proposal. Admission is pending synthetic pressure testing. No implementation, Runtime action, persona production, Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect is authorized.

## Scope

This contract defines Pit’s independent validation of a complete Foundry candidate before Guildhall Committee disposition.

## Contract

1. Pit receives only a complete, released, versioned candidate packet with required provenance and release conditions satisfied.
2. Resident testing operatives examine the candidate through bounded adversarial stations for conformance, boundary adherence, stress behavior, failure behavior, evidence sufficiency, and report integrity.
3. Pit records an examination identity and version, observations, criteria results, evidence references, uncertainty, and classified findings. Each finding identifies failure class, severity, native repair owner, and retest eligibility. Pit does not silently repair the candidate or alter source authority.
4. The examination remains independent of Foundry authorship, candidate construction, and candidate disposition. The Pit Officer preserves that independence, governs the examination, and authenticates the Pit Brief.
5. Pit may accept examination or refuse receipt when pre-examination conditions are unmet. After examination, Pit releases findings to Guildhall; it does not directly return the candidate for repair.
6. The authenticated Pit Brief identifies recommendation, repair routing, and retest eligibility; the Guildhall Committee alone determines Admit, Recycle, or Discard.
7. Pit does not determine professional fit, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, admit personas, select or reserve roster entries, recruit Operatives, or deploy.
8. A retest identifies the repaired successor version and preserves prior findings.
9. Any changed upstream input, doctrine, canon, or candidate version invalidates the affected validation result and requires a new examination.
10. Pit releases only a complete, versioned, provenance-preserving Pit Brief to Guildhall. Receipt is not approval.

## Admission Gate

Synthetic pressure testing must cover complete and incomplete receipt, examination identity/version, independence from Foundry, adversarial findings, evidence sufficiency, classified failure ownership/severity, pre-examination refusal, post-examination Guildhall routing, repair and retest, upstream invalidation, recommendation versus disposition, provenance preservation, and unauthorized boundary crossing.

This is a semantic contract only.

# DR-041 — Pit Independent Validation Contract

## Status

Active semantic contract proposal. Admission is pending synthetic pressure testing. No implementation, Runtime action, persona production, Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect is authorized.

## Scope

This contract defines Pit’s independent validation of a complete Foundry candidate before Guildhall Committee disposition.

## Contract

1. Pit receives only a complete, released, versioned candidate packet with required provenance and release conditions satisfied.
2. Resident testing operatives examine the candidate through bounded adversarial stations for conformance, boundary adherence, stress behavior, failure behavior, evidence sufficiency, and report integrity.
3. Pit records observations, criteria results, evidence references, uncertainty, failures, and retest requirements. It does not silently repair the candidate or alter source authority.
4. The Pit Officer governs and authenticates the Pit Brief.
5. Pit may accept examination, return the packet for named repair, or refuse examination when receipt conditions are unmet.
6. Pit recommends; the Guildhall Committee determines Admit, Recycle, or Discard.
7. Pit does not determine professional fit, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, admit personas, select or reserve roster entries, recruit Operatives, or deploy.
8. A retest identifies the repaired successor version and preserves prior findings.
9. Any changed upstream input, doctrine, canon, or candidate version invalidates the affected validation result and requires a new examination.
10. Pit releases only a complete, versioned, provenance-preserving Pit Brief to Guildhall. Receipt is not approval.

## Admission Gate

Synthetic pressure testing must cover complete and incomplete receipt, adversarial findings, evidence sufficiency, refusal/return, repair and retest, upstream invalidation, recommendation versus disposition, provenance preservation, and unauthorized boundary crossing.

This is a semantic contract only.

# DR-020 — Guildhall Selection Authority, Craftsman Research, and Garrison Roster Ownership

## Decision

Guildhall is the office that defines and holds the authority to determine which person or persons best fit a Castellan brief.

The Guildmaster is the Imperium Officer who occupies Guildhall and exercises that bounded authority. The Guildmaster is accountable for the selection determination.

Craftsman is the research role. Multiple Craftsman instances may be dispatched concurrently to investigate candidates, professions, achievements, traits, methods, limitations, risks, and evidence of fit. Craftsman instances present evidence-based findings; they do not select candidates or exercise Guildhall authority.

Garrison owns and maintains the roster of admitted persona specifications. The roster is a readable authoritative record. Garrison does not search, select, recommend, or act as an agent. Whichever authorized officer needs roster information reads it according to the applicable authority and brief.

## Bounded flow

`Castellan → Secretariat/Isolde → Guildmaster → Craftsman instances → Guildmaster → Garrison roster → Guildmaster → Secretariat/Isolde`

This expresses institutional communication and evidence flow, not autonomous action by offices or records.

- Guildmaster determines the relevant profession or professions and selects suitable persona specification(s) from the Garrison roster.
- Craftsman research supports the determination with evidence and provenance.
- Garrison supplies roster truth through its maintained record.
- Isolde, as Secretariat Officer, records and communicates the result through the Secretariat Acta.
- If no suitable persona exists, the matter proceeds to the authorized creation path through Guildhall, Studium, and Foundry.
- If the Castellan brief includes mission execution, the selected persona continues to Recruitment; it is not treated as an operative merely because it was selected.

## Explicit non-authority

Guildhall, Garrison, the Garrison roster, and the Secretariat Acta are not agents. Offices define responsibilities and authority; Imperium Officers act within those offices; supporting instances perform assigned work; records are read and maintained but do not decide.

## Status

This is an admitted semantic decision record. It authorizes no implementation, live persona selection, roster mutation, recruitment, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect.

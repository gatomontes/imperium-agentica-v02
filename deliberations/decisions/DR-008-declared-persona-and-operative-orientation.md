# DR-008 — Declared Persona and Operative Orientation

## Status

Recorded operator SOP decision.

This decision refines the EC-03 disposition in DR-007. It does not revise admitted Cognitive production semantics.

## Date

2026-07-24

## Decision

A bias requested by an authorized operator is a declared persona or operative orientation. It is not Imperium doctrine.

Imperium may support personas and operatives with conservative, liberal, adversarial, cautious, growth-oriented, skeptical, conciliatory, or other expressly requested orientations when the orientation is operationally specified.

Imperium does not decide that one authorized orientation is universally correct. It records the requirement, preserves its source and scope, and tests whether the resulting behavior conforms.

## Canonical Term

Imperium records the fine-tuning as a `Declared Orientation`. The operator may describe it as a bias, stance, lens, disposition, or preference.

The canonical term distinguishes an intentionally configured viewpoint from an undisclosed model tendency, an unsupported factual claim, or Imperium-wide doctrine.

## Scope Boundary

```text
Persistent across missions → Persona Specification orientation
Required only for one mission or embodiment → Operative orientation
Imperium-wide truth or doctrine → NOT CREATED
```

A mission-specific orientation must not silently mutate the Canonical Persona. It is preserved in the Operative's mission-bound configuration and provenance.

If the requested orientation requires behavior incompatible with the selected persona, Foundry or Conscription must return the mismatch rather than silently distort either artifact.

## Evidence Invariants

A Declared Orientation may shape which arguments are emphasized, which tradeoffs are preferred, what recommendations are developed, and which policy objective is optimized.

It must not:

- change an observed fact;
- invent or suppress evidence;
- fabricate, alter, or misrepresent a citation;
- convert an assumption into a fact;
- conceal material contradicting evidence required by the task;
- redefine uncertainty to obtain a preferred conclusion;
- claim that Imperium itself holds the orientation;
- override exact authority, profession, governance, refusal, or escalation boundaries.

When evidence and orientation point in different directions, the operative preserves the evidence and explains how the declared orientation affects interpretation or recommendation.

## Example

An authorized operator may require an operative to reason from a conservative rather than liberal policy orientation.

The operative may prioritize conservative premises, tradeoffs, authorities, and proposed remedies within the defined scope. It must still distinguish verified facts, disputed propositions, assumptions, and value judgments, and it must preserve material evidence that defeats a preferred factual claim.

The inverse applies to a liberal orientation or any other authorized orientation.

## Ownership

The operator originates the requirement and supplies or approves its intended meaning.

Foundry integrates a persistent persona-level orientation into a Persona Specification Candidate without inventing missing political or philosophical doctrine.

Pit tests whether the orientation is behaviorally present, bounded, internally coherent, and compatible with evidence and governance.

Garrison may preserve an admitted persona's declared orientation as inventory truth.

Conscription preserves a mission-specific operative orientation, its source, and its difference from the underlying Canonical Persona.

Studium may constrain conduct but does not originate the operator's worldview preference.

Imperium does not acquire the orientation as institutional doctrine.

## Fine-Tuning and Revision

Orientation quality is evaluated against concrete behavior, not an abstract balancing formula.

Pit findings may show that the tuning is too weak, too strong, internally contradictory, irrelevant to the work, destructive of professional competence, or in conflict with evidence duties. The responsible owner may then revise or remove the orientation through a new version.

No representativeness quota, ideological parity rule, or automatic counter-orientation is required.

## Non-Decisions

This decision does not select an orientation for any persona or mission, authorize a political doctrine, create a model fine-tuning implementation, revise CB-007, select a provider, or create or deploy an Operative.

## Supersession

This decision supersedes any interpretation of EC-03 as proposed Imperium doctrine or an abstract balancing requirement. Later domain-specific orientation contracts must preserve declared scope, authorization, provenance, evidence invariants, and versioned removal.

# DR-007 — Verifiable Language and Exact-Claim Rule

## Status

Recorded operator-wide SOP decision.

Effective for all new Imperium-authored artifacts, findings, gates, assessments, specifications, tests, and decisions.

## Date

2026-07-24

## Decision

Imperium must not use undefined evaluative labels as substitutes for facts.

A term cannot determine a finding, refusal, authorization, classification, or consequence unless its meaning is operationally defined for the exact context and its application is supported by inspectable evidence.

Imperium reasons from verifiable claims, explicit assumptions, and falsifiable criteria. It does not reason from social, political, emotional, reputational, or ideological labeling.

## Exact-Claim Contract

A consequential Imperium-authored claim must identify, as applicable:

```text
Subject:
Observed or alleged action/state:
Affected object or system:
Time and scope:
Exact evidence:
Evidence provenance:
Verification method:
Contradicting evidence:
Uncertainty:
Falsification or revision condition:
Specific consequence under evaluation:
```

If the record cannot state what occurred, how it is known, and what evidence could defeat the claim, it must remain `UNRESOLVED`.

## Prohibited Native Vocabulary

The operator explicitly rejected broad labels such as “toxic,” “harmful,” and “misinformation” as Imperium-native findings.

Those labels must not independently function as evidence, findings, Imperium-authored risk descriptions, refusal criteria, governance predicates, persona traits, test outcomes, or authorization triggers.

Replacing one rejected label with another undefined euphemism does not conform.

A precise record names the actual proposition or behavior, such as a claim contradicted by a cited primary record, a nonexistent citation, an unverifiable source, disclosure of an exactly identified datum, a measured result outside a declared tolerance, or an action violating an exact cited rule.

## External and Historical Material

Source titles, bounded quotations, imported taxonomies, and preserved historical records remain exact when fidelity requires it. This is not adoption of their vocabulary by Imperium.

Imperium-authored analysis must translate an external label into exact claims before the label can affect a finding, gate, priority, or decision. If translation is impossible, preserve it only as an attributed external assertion and leave the Imperium disposition unresolved.

Historical artifacts are not silently rewritten. This decision governs their future interpretation and all new work.

## DR-006 Pressure-Test Dispositions

### EC-01

The evidence-source standard remains a required future task. It is deferred until exemplar-grounding work approaches Human-Trait Canon construction, persona production, or admission. No EC-01 draft is authorized now.

### EC-02

The prior “living-person safeguards” framing is rejected and superseded.

The retained exact questions are whether evidence is profession-relevant, exactly sourced, verifiable, supported or disputed, necessary to the achievement-to-behavior-to-trait derivation, and subject to a stated correction condition.

These truth, provenance, relevance, and correction duties apply to evidence about every real practitioner.

### EC-03

The former exemplar-set “bias” framing is not admitted as doctrine or a current gate.

The underlying concern is deferred as later persona calibration. When a concrete persona and canon exist, Pit may test whether the selected exemplar composition produces narrow, distorted, contradictory, or ineffective professional behavior. Hagiography and Foundry may then revise the canon from preserved findings.

No quota, representativeness rule, balancing requirement, or abstract classification is authorized.

## Repository-Wide Review Rule

When an existing Imperium-authored artifact is reopened for substantive revision, replace any undefined evaluative predicate within the revised scope with an exact claim or mark it unresolved.

Do not conduct a context-free global word replacement. The semantic defect is the undefined judgment, not the character string.

## Rationale

Imperium's first mantra is `Assume nothing`.

Undefined labels conceal assumptions, permit inconsistent classification, and make conclusions difficult or impossible to falsify. Exact claims expose what is asserted and what evidence supports it.

## Non-Decisions

This decision does not declare every disputed proposition false, admit an ontology of truth, resolve future persona calibration, draft EC-01, revise CB-007, create a Runtime validator, or create or deploy a persona or Operative.

## Supersession

A later decision may add domain-specific verification or calibration standards. It may not restore undefined evaluative labels as independent evidence, findings, or decision predicates without explicit operator supersession.

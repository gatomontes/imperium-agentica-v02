# DR-013 — EC-02 Living-Person Evidence Safeguards

## Status

Recorded decision; EC-02 admitted for current semantic use.

## Date

2026-07-25

## Decision

Imperium admits EC-02 as the living-person evidence safeguard standard for exemplar grounding and Human-Trait Canon work.

EC-02 requires a necessary professional purpose, data minimization, public or authorized provenance, sensitive-information exclusion or redaction, attributed and disputed claims, correction handling, retention boundaries, and refusal or unresolved dispositions.

## Boundaries

- EC-02 is not legal advice and does not establish legal compliance.
- Public availability does not make collection necessary or appropriate.
- EC-02 does not authorize collection or processing of real-person evidence by itself; each case requires an exact professional proposition and record.
- EC-02 does not create a Saint, Human-Trait Canon entry, persona, Operative, Runtime behavior, or external effect.
- EC-01, EC-CURRENT, DR-007, DR-009, and DR-012 remain controlling.
- Synthetic fixtures remain permitted for testing.

# DR-026 — Studium Functional Chambers

## Status

Recorded semantic decision. No implementation, activation, deployment, Runtime permission, live doctrine production, or external effect is authorized.

## Decision

Studium is organized into separate functional chambers. The chambers are bounded workstations within the Studium office, not independent offices, authorities, or officers. Resident Studium operatives perform the chamber work under the Studium Officer’s governance and authentication.

The doctrinal production line is:

```text
Applicability → Boundary → Conduct → Evidence → Conflict → Issuance
```

- **Applicability Chamber** identifies which Imperium doctrine and constraints apply to the approved candidate or Officer class.
- **Boundary Chamber** defines jurisdiction, authority limits, scope, and conditions upon authority.
- **Conduct Chamber** defines duties, permitted actions, prohibitions, refusal conditions, escalation triggers, and stop conditions.
- **Evidence Chamber** defines required evidence, disclosure, uncertainty, record, and evidentiary sufficiency standards.
- **Conflict Chamber** identifies contradictions, ambiguities, omissions, and non-operable provisions; unresolved matters return to the native authority.
- **Issuance Chamber** assembles, versions, authenticates, and releases the doctrinal packet to its receiving office.

## Doctrinal classes

Studium maintains two distinct doctrinal lines:

- **Persona Governance Doctrine** → Foundry.
- **Officer Governance Doctrine** → Smith.

The chamber functions are shared as applicable, but the two doctrinal classes, inputs, outputs, authority boundaries, and downstream consumers must not be collapsed.

Studium defines evidence requirements for its doctrine. It does not become the owner of Imperium-wide proof, provenance, audit, or artifact systems.

## Boundaries

Guildhall determines professional and exemplar fit. Collegium determines durable advisory-role requirements. Studium authors governance doctrine. Foundry and Smith embody their respective doctrinal classes. Pit and Spur test their respective candidates. Studium does not forge, test, admit, recruit, activate, or deploy.

A conformant doctrinal packet permits downstream review only; it grants no authority and triggers no Runtime transition.

## Governing principle

> Studium separates the doctrinal questions so each remains inspectable: applicability, boundaries, conduct, evidence, conflict, and issuance.

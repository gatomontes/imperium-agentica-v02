# DR-019 — Office, Officeholder, and Institutional Record Distinction

## Decision

An Imperium office is the institutional authority and responsibility-bearing structure. An authorized Imperium Officer occupies that office and performs its bounded functions. The officer does not acquire authority beyond the office’s defined authority by occupying it.

This rule applies to Secretariat, Castellan, Guildhall, Studium, Foundry, Garrison, and every other Imperium office.

## Secretariat application

Secretariat is the operator-facing office. Its officer receives the operator, preserves the request, brings it before the appropriate authority, carries determinations to the responsible office, maintains correspondence and status, and returns outcomes. Secretariat does not determine the request merely because it receives or records it.

Castellan determines whether a request requires persona production, persona recruitment, mission execution, or a combination. Secretariat brings the request and Castellan’s determination between the operator and the responsible offices.

## Acta and Office Specification

The Secretariat Acta is the authoritative chronological, provenance-linked record of Secretariat activity and correspondence. It records requests, matters brought before authorities, determinations as received, instructions, communications, handoffs, artifacts, status, exceptions, unresolved matters, and final disposition. It does not replace the office design.

The Secretariat Office Specification defines the office: purpose, placement, authority, officeholder role, functions, boundaries, workflows, records, inputs, outputs, handoffs, and proof/provenance/ownership requirements.

## Isolde

Isolde is the designated Imperium Officer for Secretariat. She maintains the Secretariat Acta and performs Secretariat’s bounded office functions. Isolde is not the Secretary persona requested by an operator; that persona remains a separate possible product of the persona-production workflow.

## Boundary

This decision records semantics only. It does not authorize Secretariat implementation, Isolde activation, persona production, recruitment, deployment, Runtime action, credentials, or external effect.

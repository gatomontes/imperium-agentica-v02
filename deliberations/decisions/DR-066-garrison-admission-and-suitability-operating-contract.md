# DR-066 — Garrison Admission and Suitability Operating Contract

## Status

Synthetic semantic admission review. Admitted for future implementation design and synthetic testing only. No live Persona, Operative, roster, activation, deployment, credential, Runtime, provider, or external effect is authorized.

## Function

Garrison remembers the admitted Persona inventory and determines whether a suitable Persona exists for a Work Specification. It does not forge Personas, alter doctrine, create Operatives, or select execution instruments. Recruitment receives an exact admitted Persona and later converts it into a mission-specific Operative.

## Contract

Admission requires an exact correlated Work Specification; immutable Persona Specification version; complete Foundry/Pit lineage and disposition; applicable Studium doctrine and evidence standard; explicit admission authority and timestamp; and no unresolved conflict, invalidation, quarantine, or supersession. Inventory identity, version, provenance, admission disposition, status, and removal history are preserved.

Suitability evaluates required capability and profession fit, doctrine compatibility, evidence sufficiency, current validity, and—when named—execution-instrument fit under CTRL-004. Failure of any required criterion is not suitable. Reservation is not selection: reservation records only an availability hold and expiry. Selection is a separate disposition naming exact Persona and Work Specification versions, criteria result, authority, and time. Garrison may return NO_SUITABLE_PERSONA, UNRESOLVED, or REFUSED rather than manufacture fit.

Changes to the Work Specification, doctrine, evidence, Persona version, capability claim, or execution instrument invalidate affected suitability and require re-evaluation. Superseded, withdrawn, quarantined, decanonized, or removed Personas cannot be newly selected. Garrison does not silently repair inputs, compose incompatible versions, bypass Pit disposition, override Studium boundaries, or convert recommendation into selection.

## Synthetic admission

The accompanying review records 18/18 bounded cases passing across admission, inventory, suitability, reservation/selection separation, refusal, invalidation, supersession, removal, provenance, and CTRL-004 instrument-fit handling.

This decision does not authorize implementation or operational use.

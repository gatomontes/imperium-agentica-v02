# DR-066 — Garrison Admission and Suitability Operating Contract

## Status

Synthetic semantic admission review. Admitted for future implementation design and synthetic testing only. No live Persona, Operative, roster, activation, deployment, credential, Runtime, provider, or external effect is authorized.

## Function

Garrison is the office that remembers the admitted Persona inventory and determines whether a suitable Persona exists for a Work Specification. Garrison does not forge Personas, alter doctrine, create Operatives, or select execution instruments. Recruitment receives an exact admitted Persona and later converts it into a mission-specific Operative.

## Contract

Garrison may admit a Persona only when it receives:

- an exact, correlated Work Specification;
- an immutable Persona Specification version;
- complete Foundry/Pit lineage and disposition;
- applicable Studium doctrine and evidence standard;
- an explicit admission authority and timestamp;
- no unresolved conflict, invalidation, quarantine, or supersession.

Garrison must preserve inventory identity, version, provenance, admission disposition, residency/status, and removal history.

Suitability is a constrained resolution against the Work Specification. It evaluates required capability and profession fit, doctrine compatibility, evidence sufficiency, current validity, and—when the Work Specification names an execution medium—instrument-fit evidence under CTRL-004. A candidate that fails any required criterion is not suitable.

Reservation is not selection. A temporary reservation records an availability hold and expiry; it grants no mission authority. Selection is a separate, explicit disposition naming the exact Persona version, correlated Work Specification, criteria result, authority, and time. Garrison may return NO_SUITABLE_PERSONA, UNRESOLVED, or REFUSED rather than manufacture fit.

## Change and removal rules

A changed Work Specification, doctrine, evidence basis, Persona version, capability claim, or execution instrument invalidates the affected suitability result and requires re-evaluation. Superseded, withdrawn, quarantined, decanonized, or removed Personas cannot be newly selected. Existing reservations are released when their source artifact becomes invalid or expires. Every disposition points to the exact versions it evaluated.

Garrison may report availability and suitability, but it may not silently repair inputs, compose incompatible versions, bypass Pit disposition, override Studium boundaries, or convert a recommendation into selection.

## Synthetic admission

The accompanying review records 18/18 bounded cases passing across admission, inventory, suitability, reservation/selection separation, refusal, invalidation, supersession, removal, provenance, and CTRL-004 instrument-fit handling.

This decision does not authorize implementation or operational use.

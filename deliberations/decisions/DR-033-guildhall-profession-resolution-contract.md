# DR-033 — Guildhall Profession Resolution Contract

## Status

Recorded semantic contract proposal for independent review. It authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, live persona or Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect.

## Boundary

Guildhall determines the professional pattern required by an approved Work Specification. It does not define mission intent, author Persona Governance Doctrine, select or reserve a persona, forge or test a persona, or recruit/deploy an Operative.

## Contract

Guildhall receives an approved Work Specification and produces a versioned Profession Specification and Resolution Assessment. The assessment must preserve the reasoning, evidence, competence boundaries, adjacent and excluded professions, unresolved ambiguity, and provenance used to reach the result.

Guildhall must refuse or return work when the Work Specification is missing, contradictory, outside its professional-resolution authority, or insufficient to distinguish among legitimate professional patterns. It may request decomposition or clarification without silently rewriting the work.

Where multiple professions remain legitimate, Guildhall must preserve the candidate set and explicit comparison. It may not silently break a tie, select a persona, or reserve Garrison inventory. No-match and unresolved outcomes remain explicit outputs.

Reassessment is required when the approved Work Specification, Mission Need, material constraints, evidence basis, or relevant professional assumptions change.

## Review Gate

Pressure-test refusal, decomposition, ambiguity, multi-profession tie, reassessment, artifact ownership, and Garrison-search eligibility. Admission requires a separate review record. Synthetic fixtures only; no real-person data or live production is authorized.

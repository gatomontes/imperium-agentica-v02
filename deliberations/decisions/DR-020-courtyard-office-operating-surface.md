# DR-020 — Courtyard Office Operating Surface

## Status

Recorded decision. Semantic architecture only.

## Decision

The Courtyard is Imperium's operating surface for its offices and the Imperium Officers who occupy them.

An office is an institutional function and bounded authority. An Imperium Officer occupies the office and exercises that office's authority within its limits. The Courtyard is the surface on which those officers conduct institutional work; the Courtyard does not itself become an officeholder, authority, or decision-maker.

## Scope

The Courtyard provides the operating context in which Imperium Officers may:

- receive and act on authoritative assignments;
- exercise the bounded authority of their occupied offices;
- exchange requests, determinations, artifacts, evidence, and status;
- maintain office records, including the Secretariat Acta;
- bring matters to other offices and receive their determinations;
- interact with Runtime as the underlying operating machinery, subject to separately defined permissions and controls.

Examples include Isolde occupying Secretariat, the Castellan Officer occupying Castellan, and the Guildhall officers and Craftsmen conducting authorized institutional work.

## Boundary relationship

The Courtyard is distinct from external mission deployment.

- **Courtyard:** Imperium offices and their Officers operate here.
- **La Cortine:** an authorized boundary crossed by an operative or research instance requiring access to an external surface.
- **Lazaretto:** the compulsory return and quarantine path for returning field material.
- **External research/deployment surface:** where an authorized Journeyman may retrieve internet content or otherwise conduct bounded external activity.

A Journeyman may originate in the Courtyard, cross La Cortine only under the required authority and permission, and return through Lazaretto. Isolde and other officeholders ordinarily perform their office functions in the Courtyard and do not cross La Cortine merely to conduct office work.

## Authority and records

The Courtyard does not grant authority by itself. Office authority remains defined by the relevant office specification and exercised by its authorized Imperium Officer. Runtime access, deployment authorization, credential use, provider access, and external effect remain separate decisions.

The Courtyard's institutional activity must be recorded through the applicable office artifacts and provenance requirements. Secretariat's operational record is the Secretariat Acta; the building plan for Secretariat remains its separate Secretariat Office Specification.

## Current boundary

This decision records terminology and architectural placement only. It does not authorize Courtyard implementation, officer activation, office instantiation, Runtime action, permission grants, credential work, Journeyman research, La Cortine crossing, deployment, or external effect.

## Supersession

This decision does not supersede the separate office, authority, provenance, Runtime, deployment, or return-path decisions. It establishes the Courtyard as their proposed internal operating surface and leaves its detailed contracts for a separately authorized review.

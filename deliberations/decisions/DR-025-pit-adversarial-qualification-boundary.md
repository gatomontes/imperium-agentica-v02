# DR-025 — Pit Adversarial Qualification Boundary

## Status

Recorded semantic decision. No implementation, Runtime activation, live persona production, deployment, or external effect is authorized.

## Decision

Pit is Imperium's adversarial qualification office. It receives the complete Persona Specification Candidate from Foundry, subjects it to hostile and difficult examination, records the results, and returns an authenticated Pit Brief to the Guildhall Committee.

The sequence is:

```text
Foundry → Pit → Guildhall Committee
       candidate   Pit Brief
```

## Stations

Pit may be organized into bounded functional stations occupied by resident testing operatives:

- **Receiving:** verifies the candidate, lineage, and test inputs.
- **Contrarian:** attacks assumptions and searches for contradictions.
- **Boundary:** tests duties, prohibitions, authority, refusal, and scope limits.
- **Stress:** applies difficult, ambiguous, and adversarial scenarios.
- **Failure:** identifies unsafe behavior, drift, omissions, and collapse conditions.
- **Evidence:** records tests, results, artifacts, and uncertainty.
- **Briefing:** composes the versioned Pit Brief.

A Pit Officer governs the candidate's passage, ensures the examination is appropriate, preserves independence from Foundry, and authenticates the brief. Resident testing operatives perform the bounded intelligent work.

## Product and disposition boundary

Pit produces findings and a recommendation, not an admission decision. The Pit Brief may report:

- passed;
- passed with weaknesses;
- failed with recycling targets;
- fundamentally unsound.

The Guildhall Committee retains disposition authority over the tested candidate:

- **Admit** — approve entry to the admitted-persona roster;
- **Recycle** — return to Foundry with required corrective targets;
- **Discard** — terminate that production attempt.

Garrison remains custodian of the admitted-persona roster. It does not test, select, or admit.

## Non-authority

Pit must not:

- rewrite or repair the candidate it tests;
- admit the candidate;
- replace Guildhall, Studium, Hagiography, or Foundry authority;
- recruit, activate, or deploy an operative;
- treat fluency or task accuracy as proof of governance;
- conceal failures or overwrite historical findings.

> Foundry forges. Pit attacks and records. Guildhall determines disposition. Garrison keeps the admitted roster.

This is a semantic decision only. It does not authorize live research, persona production, implementation, Runtime action, deployment, or external effect.

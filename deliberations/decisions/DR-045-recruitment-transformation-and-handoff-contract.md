# DR-045 — Recruitment/Conscription Transformation and Handoff Contract

## Status

Proposal for semantic review. This record authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, live data, persona or Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect.

## Contract

Recruitment/Conscription packages an admitted, versioned Persona Specification into a deployment-medium-specific Operative asset. It does not determine profession, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, forge or validate the Persona Specification, alter Garrison roster truth, or decide Committee disposition.

Recruitment may receive only a complete handoff packet containing the admitted Persona Specification, the complete eligible Persona Specification set and its authoritative versions, Committee disposition and authorized selection criterion, the recorded selection/tie outcome, Pit evidence, Guildhall suitability finding, Garrison roster identity/state, required deployment-medium constraints, provenance, and immutable source versions. Conscription selects among the eligible admitted Persona Specifications only under the recorded authorized criterion; unresolved ties are refused/returned rather than silently resolved. Receipt is not authorization to activate or deploy.

Recruitment verifies completeness, authority, compatibility with the declared deployment medium, provenance, version integrity, and the declared selection/tie outcome. It refuses or returns incomplete, contradictory, stale, invalidated, or boundary-crossing packets without silently repairing or substituting inputs.

A successful transformation produces a versioned, deployment-medium-specific Operative Package that preserves source identity, source versions, professional competence, applicable governance doctrine, canonical traits, refusal and escalation behavior, boundaries, transformation record, and provenance. The package includes preservation findings for each required capability and behavior; any forced deviation is explicitly recorded, dispositioned, and cannot be treated as successful without authorized acceptance. Transformation does not activate, mission-bind, deploy, access credentials, or create external effects. Successor packages are required after any material upstream change.

Recruitment releases the package only to the separately authorized downstream function and records a handoff state. The downstream recipient must return an acceptance finding before the package is considered accepted. A rejection or unresolved limitation returns the package with its finding to Recruitment for disposition; Recruitment may revise only through a new immutable successor package, or refuse/route the packet to the responsible upstream authority when the issue is upstream. No rejected or unresolved package may be treated as accepted or released onward.

Recruitment owns packaging, transformation, preservation, deviation, and handoff evidence; Garrison owns roster state; Muster/Spur and later deployment authorities retain their separate boundaries.

## Boundaries

Recruitment/Conscription does not select by professional fit, admit candidates, reserve roster entries, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, forge Persona Specifications, independently validate candidates, activate, deploy, or produce external effects.

## Admission gate

Admission requires synthetic pressure testing of complete/incomplete packets, eligible-set selection and tie handling, authority separation, medium compatibility, preservation and deviation findings, provenance, refusal/return, immutable successor lineage, transformation and handoff evidence, acceptance/rejection routing, and the non-activation/non-deployment boundary.

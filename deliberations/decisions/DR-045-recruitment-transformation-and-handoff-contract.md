# DR-045 — Recruitment/Conscription Transformation and Handoff Contract

## Status

Proposal for semantic review. This record authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, live data, persona or Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect.

## Contract

Recruitment/Conscription packages an admitted, versioned Persona Specification into a deployment-medium-specific Operative asset. It does not determine profession, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, forge or validate the Persona Specification, alter Garrison roster truth, or decide Committee disposition.

Recruitment may receive only a complete handoff packet containing the admitted Persona Specification, Committee disposition, Pit evidence, Guildhall suitability finding, Garrison roster identity/state, required deployment-medium constraints, provenance, and immutable source versions. Receipt is not authorization to activate or deploy.

Recruitment verifies completeness, authority, compatibility with the declared deployment medium, provenance, and version integrity. It refuses or returns incomplete, contradictory, stale, invalidated, or boundary-crossing packets without silently repairing or substituting inputs.

A successful transformation produces a versioned, deployment-medium-specific Operative Package that preserves source identity, source versions, transformation record, boundaries, and provenance. Transformation does not activate, mission-bind, deploy, access credentials, or create external effects. Successor packages are required after any material upstream change.

Recruitment releases the package only to the separately authorized downstream function. It owns packaging and transformation evidence; Garrison owns roster state; Muster/Spur and later deployment authorities retain their separate boundaries.

## Boundaries

Recruitment/Conscription does not select by professional fit, admit candidates, reserve roster entries, author doctrine, canonize exemplars, forge Persona Specifications, independently validate candidates, activate, deploy, or produce external effects.

## Admission gate

Admission requires synthetic pressure testing of complete/incomplete packets, authority separation, medium compatibility, provenance, refusal/return, immutable successor lineage, transformation evidence, and the non-activation/non-deployment boundary.

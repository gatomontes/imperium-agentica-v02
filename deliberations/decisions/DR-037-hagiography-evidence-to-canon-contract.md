# DR-037 — Hagiography Evidence-to-Canon Contract

## Status

Active semantic contract proposal. Admission requires a separate synthetic pressure test and admission review. No implementation, Runtime action, live-person collection, persona production, Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect is authorized.

## Scope

Hagiography receives professionally relevant evidence about an exemplary real-world practitioner only with an exact Guildhall approval/exemplar-fit input. It operates under EC-CURRENT and the Human-Trait Canon schema. It converts sufficiently supported evidence into a versioned Canon entry candidate for downstream use by Guildhall and Foundry.

Absent the required Guildhall approval/exemplar-fit input, Hagiography must refuse, return, or defer the material rather than process it as an approved exemplar.

## Contract

Hagiography must:

1. verify source sufficiency, provenance, professional relevance, and achievement claims;
2. distinguish observed evidence, reported claims, interpretation, and derived traits;
3. record uncertainty, contradiction, dispute, correction, and evidence gaps;
4. derive only transferable professional traits supported by the evidence;
5. preserve source-to-claim-to-trait lineage;
6. identify the owning authority and version each Canon entry candidate;
7. refuse, return, or defer material gaps rather than silently completing them;
8. expose supersession, invalidation, and downstream impact without erasing prior versions;
9. issue a canonization recommendation that states the evidence basis, residual uncertainty, and any conditions for admission.

Hagiography must not determine profession fit, approve Guildhall candidates, forge Persona Specifications, admit Operatives, or authorize deployment.

## Outputs

The bounded output is a versioned Human-Trait Canon entry candidate with evidence references, claim classifications, derived-trait rationale, uncertainty, contradiction/dispute state, provenance, ownership, canonization recommendation, and downstream handoff status.

Hagiography owns the transition gate for its evidence record: it may mark the entry canonized only when the approval/exemplar-fit input is present, the evidence standard is satisfied, material contradictions are resolved or explicitly accepted, and its canonization recommendation is recorded. Otherwise the entry remains pending, returned, refused, or superseded. A candidate is never canonized by receipt, and a downstream office may consume only the canonized entry.

Receipt by a downstream office is not approval. Guildhall retains profession-resolution authority; Studium governs the admitted Persona Governance Doctrine branch; Foundry retains Persona Specification production authority.

## Review Gate

Pressure testing must cover missing Guildhall approval/exemplar-fit input, insufficient or conflicting evidence, unverifiable achievement, non-transferable trait inference, privacy or relevance boundary, correction and supersession, ownership, refusal/return, canonization recommendation and gate, and downstream handoff.

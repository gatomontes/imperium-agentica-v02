# DR-047 — Cross-Chain Invalidation and Version-Integrity Contract

## Status

Proposal for semantic review. This record authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, live data, persona or Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect.

## Contract

Cross-chain invalidation governs how a material defect, withdrawal, supersession, or invalidation in one creation-apparatus artifact affects dependent artifacts downstream. It does not create a new authority office or replace the issuing office’s disposition authority.

Every dependent artifact identifies its exact upstream artifact versions, dependency relationship, validity state, and provenance. A material upstream change creates a new successor version or invalidates the dependent artifact; it never silently mutates an admitted artifact in place.

The office that owns the invalidated artifact records the invalidation reason, evidence, effective status, scope, and successor condition. Downstream custodians quarantine or mark dependent artifacts non-usable when they receive an authoritative invalidation notice, preserve the prior artifact and provenance, and prevent further handoff or acceptance until dependency review completes.

Invalidation propagates through the dependency graph to every affected downstream artifact, including Persona Specifications, roster entries, Operative Packages, and handoff records. Unaffected artifacts remain valid only when their independence is explicitly established and recorded. Uncertainty is a hold/refusal condition, not implicit validity.

Each downstream office performs only its bounded response: Studium/Foundry revise doctrine or Persona Specifications; Pit re-examines affected candidates; Guildhall Committee determines candidate disposition; Garrison updates roster truth; Recruitment/Conscription withdraws or creates successor packages; Muster and Spur remain outside this contract’s activation/deployment authority.

Revalidation produces an explicit finding, new immutable version where required, and provenance link to the invalidation event. A rejected or unresolved revalidation returns to the responsible upstream authority and cannot be released onward. Conflicting invalidation notices are escalated to the designated authority; no office resolves the conflict by assumption.

## Boundaries

This contract does not authorize implementation, Runtime behavior, live collection, candidate selection, activation, deployment, credential use, external effects, or unilateral cross-office authority transfer.

## Admission gate

Admission requires synthetic pressure testing of direct and transitive invalidation, supersession, partial dependency, stale notice, conflicting notice, quarantine, unaffected-artifact determination, successor creation, revalidation, refusal/return, provenance, and non-activation boundaries.

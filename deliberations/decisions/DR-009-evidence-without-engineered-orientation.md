# DR-009 — Evidence Without Engineered Orientation

## Status

Recorded operator-wide SOP decision.

This decision supersedes DR-008.

## Date

2026-07-24

## Decision

No persona or operative may be tuned toward a preferred political, ideological, philosophical, social, or conclusion-seeking orientation.

Imperium must provide the cleanest attainable account of the available evidence. The operator alone decides what value, preference, ideology, or personal judgment to apply afterward.

Imperium's duty is to preserve reality as accurately as its evidence permits, including contradiction, uncertainty, missing information, and limits.

## Boundary Maxim

> It is what it is.

Operationally: Imperium must not alter what the evidence supports in order to make the result more desirable, agreeable, persuasive, balanced, comforting, or ideologically aligned.

## Clean Evidence Product

A conformant evidence product distinguishes:

```text
Verified observations
Source claims
Disputed propositions
Contradicting evidence
Explicit assumptions
Unknown or missing information
Measurement and source limitations
Inferences and their derivation
Confidence and revision conditions
```

Imperium must not disguise any one category as another.

“Cleanest attainable” does not mean certainty, neutrality by omission, or equal weighting of unequal evidence. It means the record is as exact, complete, provenance-preserving, and free from engineered conclusion preference as the authorized evidence permits.

## Prohibited Tuning

Imperium must not configure a persona or operative to:

- favor conservative, liberal, progressive, nationalist, religious, secular, partisan, or other ideological conclusions;
- select or suppress evidence to support a preferred answer;
- change weighting because a result is politically or emotionally preferred;
- manufacture artificial parity between claims with materially different evidence;
- omit material contradiction to protect the operator from an unwelcome result;
- present advocacy as disinterested analysis;
- treat an operator's desired outcome as evidence.

## Permitted Scope and Perspective

An operator may define the question, domain, jurisdiction, time horizon, objective, constraints, acceptance criteria, and requested output.

Imperium may accurately research or explain a named viewpoint as an object of study—for example, “What arguments do conservative advocates make?”—provided it attributes the viewpoint, verifies factual claims independently, preserves contradiction, and does not adopt that viewpoint as the operative's own orientation.

Professional personas may still possess evidenced traits such as rigor, skepticism, clarity, persistence, restraint, or decisiveness. Those traits govern method and behavior; they must not preselect substantive conclusions.

## Operator Authority After Handoff

The operator may accept, reject, disregard, reinterpret, or act contrary to Imperium's evidence product within the operator's own authority.

Imperium does not sanitize the evidence in anticipation of that choice and does not acquire responsibility for preferences the operator applies after handoff.

Applicable authority, provenance, and decision records still preserve what Imperium provided and what later decision was made.

## Ownership Consequences

Hagiography may canonize evidenced professional traits but not ideological conclusion preferences.

Foundry must not integrate a Declared Orientation of the kind authorized by DR-008.

Pit must test for evidence selection, conclusion steering, contradiction suppression, artificial parity, and advocacy presented as analysis.

Garrison must not admit a persona whose specification contains engineered ideological or conclusion-seeking orientation.

Conscription must not add mission-specific viewpoint bias to an admitted persona.

Curia and the authorized operator may deliberate and decide from the preserved evidence; their decision does not retroactively alter the evidence product.

## Relationship to DR-007

DR-007 remains controlling. Exact claims and verifiable language are necessary to produce the clean evidence product required here.

DR-009 adds the prohibition against engineered conclusion preference.

## Disposition of EC-03

EC-03 is closed as a proposed Imperium concern.

No exemplar balancing doctrine, declared orientation, ideological fine-tuning, or later bias-calibration program is required. Concrete Pit evidence may still reveal that a persona omits facts, suppresses contradiction, or steers conclusions; those are exact conformance defects under DR-007 and DR-009.

## Supersession of DR-008

DR-008 remains historical evidence of a considered and rejected alternative.

Its permission for operator-requested persona or operative orientation is withdrawn. No work may rely on DR-008 as current authority.

## Non-Decisions

This decision does not remove professional judgment, mission scope, explicit objectives, evidentiary weighting, uncertainty assessment, or operator authority after handoff.

It does not revise CB-007 production, create a Runtime validator, select a model or provider, or create or deploy a persona or Operative.

## Supersession

A later decision may refine evidence presentation or testing. Restoring engineered political, ideological, or conclusion-seeking orientation requires explicit operator supersession of this decision.

# DR-019 — Guildhall Deliberative Committee

## Status

Recorded decision. Semantic clarification only.

## Decision

Guildhall’s profession and exemplar determination is exercised by a committee, not unilaterally by one Guildmaster.

The committee is composed of Craftsmen. Journeymen are dispatched as research instances to gather, verify, compare, and present evidence.

The Guildmaster is the presiding Guildhall Officer. The Guildmaster convenes or orders the proceedings, ensures the required deliberative roles are heard, maintains procedural integrity, records or authenticates the committee’s determination, and does not replace the committee’s collective judgment.

## Deliberative seats

The committee may use diverse analytical seats, including:

- Contrarian — challenges assumptions and seeks disqualifying or contrary evidence.
- Advocate — presents the strongest defensible case for a profession or exemplar.
- Outsider — tests the choice from outside the committee’s dominant frame.
- Legalist — examines authority, boundaries, obligations, and unacceptable risks.
- Purist — tests fidelity to the actual profession rather than superficial resemblance.
- Synthesist — reconciles findings, identifies composites, and marks unresolved conflicts.

These are analytical lenses, not permanent biases or required conclusions. Each seat must expose its reasoning and evidence.

## Determination scope

The Guildhall Committee may determine:

- the profession or professions required by the Castellan brief;
- the human exemplar or exemplars most suitable for the professional foundation;
- whether one exemplar is sufficient;
- whether a composite is justified;
- whether evidence is sufficient;
- whether uncertainty requires clarification or return to Castellan.

Garrison owns and exposes the admitted-persona roster. Garrison does not search, evaluate, select, or admit personas.

## Authority and records

Journeymen research and report. Craftsmen deliberate and determine. The Guildmaster presides and authenticates. The committee’s reasoning, disagreements, evidence, uncertainty, and final determination must be preserved as a versioned Guildhall artifact.

## Current boundary

This decision admits terminology and institutional semantics only. It does not authorize live committee dispatch, real-person research, persona production, Garrison mutation, recruitment, implementation, deployment, Runtime action, or external effect.

## Supersession

This decision supersedes the unilateral Guildmaster selection model for Guildhall profession/exemplar determination. It does not supersede the separate boundaries governing Garrison ownership, evidence standards, persona production, or execution.

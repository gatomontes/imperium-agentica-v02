# DR-039 — Foundry Input and Conflict Contract

## Status

Active semantic contract proposal. Admission requires a separate synthetic pressure test. No implementation, Runtime action, persona production, Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect is authorized.

## Scope

Foundry receives the complete, approved input packet required by the admitted creation chain: the Work Specification and authorized patterns or constraints; Guildhall’s approved profession-resolution package and resolution assessment; applicable Persona Governance Doctrine from Studium; Hagiography’s canonized Human-Trait Canon entry and EC disposition or safeguards record; relevant provenance findings; operator constraints; and any other material, identified upstream artifact required by the approved contract. It integrates only satisfied, versioned inputs into a Persona Specification Candidate.

## Contract

1. Foundry accepts only identified, versioned, provenance-preserving inputs whose upstream gates are satisfied.
2. Guildhall’s approved Profession Specification and resolution assessment establish the professional target and retain profession-resolution authority.
3. Studium’s applicable Persona Governance Doctrine establishes permitted conduct, evidence, boundaries, and required constraints.
4. Hagiography’s canonized Human-Trait Canon entry supplies supported transferable traits; its EC disposition or safeguards record governs admissibility. Candidate or rejected entries are not acceptable inputs.
5. The Work Specification, authorized patterns or constraints, operator constraints, provenance findings, and other material upstream artifacts remain required when applicable; Foundry may not omit them merely because the three principal institutional sources are present.
6. Source authority follows domain ownership: no input source may silently override another office’s authority.
7. Conflicts are recorded explicitly with the affected claims, versions, provenance, and governing owner. Foundry may reconcile only within its synthesis boundary; unresolved conflicts are returned to the responsible upstream office.
8. Missing, stale, contradictory, or unauthenticated inputs cause refusal or named return, never silent substitution or inference.
9. The resulting Persona Specification Candidate preserves source lineage, transformation rationale, unresolved matters, version identity, dependency identity, and the exact intended downstream recipient.
10. Revision or supersession of an upstream input invalidates the affected candidate. Any conformant reintegration creates a new immutable candidate version with new dependency lineage; the prior candidate and any downstream qualification result remain historical and cannot be mutated or reused as the successor.
11. Foundry hands Pit only a complete candidate satisfying release conditions. Pit’s qualification authority remains distinct from Foundry’s synthesis authority.

## Boundaries

Foundry does not determine professional fit, author Persona Governance Doctrine, canonize human traits, perform adversarial qualification, select a Canonical Persona, reserve Garrison inventory, recruit an Operative, activate, deploy, or create external effects.

This proposal is semantic only; admission and implementation require separate authorization.

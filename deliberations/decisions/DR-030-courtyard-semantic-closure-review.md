# DR-030 — Courtyard Semantic Closure Review

## Status

Recorded semantic decision. This review closes the Courtyard institutional-definition step at the semantic boundary. It authorizes no implementation, Runtime action, activation, deployment, credential use, live data, or external effect.

## Closure Finding

Courtyard is semantically complete as Imperium’s office operating surface. Its admitted contract now defines:

- membership and institutional placement;
- the distinction between offices, Officers, stations, and resident operatives;
- office and station authority boundaries;
- artifact release, receipt, refusal, return, repair, escalation, and closure;
- artifact identity, version, status, provenance, custody, and authorship;
- station lifecycle and rework traceability;
- failure detection, attribution, recovery, and accountability;
- non-boundaries against Curia, La Cortine, Lazaretto, Garrison, Muster, Recruitment/Conscription, and Runtime.

Courtyard composes these contracts but does not become an authority source, mission decision-maker, credential holder, quarantine function, roster custodian, deployment surface, or Runtime operator.

## Completion Boundary

Courtyard semantic definition is complete for the current architectural track. Remaining questions are implementation and operationalization questions, including concrete schemas, persistence, transport, enforcement, Runtime behavior, activation, deployment, credentials, live providers, and external effects. Those questions require a separately authorized step and do not reopen this semantic closure.

## Gate

The Courtyard closure is semantic and architectural only. No implementation, Runtime action, activation, deployment, credential use, live data, or external effect is authorized.

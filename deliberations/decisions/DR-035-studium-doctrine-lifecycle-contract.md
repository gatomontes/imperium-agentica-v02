# DR-035 — Studium Doctrine Lifecycle Contract

## Status

Semantic contract proposal. Pressure testing and admission remain separate. Synthetic evidence only. No implementation, Runtime action, live persona or Operative creation, activation, deployment, credential use, or external effect is authorized.

## Boundary

Studium is the doctrinal office. It receives a defined institutional subject and determines which admitted doctrine applies, what boundaries and conduct rules govern the subject, what evidence standard is required, how conflicts are exposed, and what versioned doctrinal artifact must be issued to the receiving office.

Studium does not determine professional fit, select personas, approve Guildhall candidates, forge Persona Specifications, test candidates, reserve Garrison inventory, recruit Operatives, or deploy them. Guildhall’s approved candidate is a precondition for doctrine fitting.

## Functional chambers

The lifecycle is performed through bounded functional chambers under the Studium Officer:

1. Applicability — identify the subject, scope, and applicable doctrine set.
2. Boundary — state permitted, prohibited, required, and unresolved conduct.
3. Conduct — express operational do/do-not requirements without inventing authority.
4. Evidence — specify required evidence, sufficiency, provenance, uncertainty, and correction conditions.
5. Conflict — expose contradictions, gaps, supersession, and unresolved interpretation; do not silently reconcile them.
6. Issuance — authenticate and release a versioned doctrine artifact with scope, dependencies, status, and receiving office.

A chamber is a function performed by resident station operatives; it is not an Officer or an independent authority source.

## Lifecycle rules

- Intake must identify the subject, requesting authority, input versions, intended receiving office, and decision needed; incomplete or out-of-scope intake is refused or returned.
- Applicability must be explicit. No doctrine is applied merely because it is nearby, familiar, or convenient.
- Boundary and conduct statements must distinguish requirement, permission, prohibition, uncertainty, and evidence condition.
- Evidence requirements must be inspectable and must preserve provenance; absence of evidence is not approval.
- Conflicts remain explicit and route to the named authority for disposition. Studium may not silently choose a winner or create authority.
- Issuance requires a complete, versioned, status-bearing artifact and named recipient. Draft, rejected, superseded, and invalidated artifacts are not current doctrine.
- Material source, scope, assumption, evidence, or governing-doctrine change triggers reassessment; it does not silently mutate an issued artifact.
- Invalidation and supersession preserve prior versions, reason, authority, and affected downstream dependencies.

## Ownership and refusal

Studium owns doctrine-lifecycle artifacts and their doctrinal provenance. The receiving office owns application within its authority and must acknowledge, reject, or return an artifact that is incomplete, conflicting, expired, or inapplicable. Receipt is not approval.

The Studium Officer governs and authenticates Studium work. Resident operatives perform bounded chamber work and may not adjudicate conflicts, issue doctrine, or cross chambers on their own authority.

## Separation of doctrinal lines

Persona Governance Doctrine is directed to Foundry. Officer Governance Doctrine is directed to Smith. A Studium artifact must identify its doctrinal line and may not silently substitute one for the other.

Admission requires synthetic pressure testing of intake, applicability, boundaries, conduct, evidence, conflict, issuance, revision, invalidation, and receiving-office refusal behavior.

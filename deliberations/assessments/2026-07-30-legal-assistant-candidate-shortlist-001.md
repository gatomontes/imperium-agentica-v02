### Merit criteria

The provisional criteria for this legal-assistant proposition are:

1. **Professional relevance** — direct relevance to legal-assistant work.
2. **Demonstrated excellence** — specific, observable, professionally documented achievement.
3. **Trait coverage** — evidence covering the required trait cluster without unsupported inference.
4. **Evidence quality** — authoritative, attributable, inspectable, and sufficiently detailed sources.
5. **Transferability** — evidence that the achievement reflects a repeatable professional practice,
   not merely an isolated honor or title.
6. **Evidence sufficiency** — enough material to support a bounded review while respecting minimization.

Each criterion must receive an evidence-linked assessment and uncertainty notation. A candidate may
be returned, refused, or escalated if the evidence is insufficient, conflicting, disputed, or overly
dependent on a single unsupported claim. A higher score does not override a refusal condition.

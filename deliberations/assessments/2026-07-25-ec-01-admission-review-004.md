## Boundary Review

EC-01 is an evidence standard for the derivation of a bounded trait candidate. It does not:

- declare a person exemplary in full;
- establish moral, legal, medical, or psychological conclusions;
- treat source count, reputation, rank, or fame as truth;
- create a Human-Trait Canon;
- authorize persona or Officer construction;
- create a production gate, Runtime behavior, or external effect by itself.

## Review Finding

# Operative Creation and Selection Deliberation Scope 001

## Date

2026-07-24

## Status

Active bounded deliberation scope.

## Purpose

Review the already-admitted Operative creation and selection process against Imperium's current deliberation records before proposing any semantic or implementation revision.

This is an assurance review, not a reconstruction of Track A.

## Admitted Process Under Review

| Stage | Current owner | Present product or decision |
|---|---|---|
| mission formation | Castellan | Mission Need and approved Work Specification |
| profession resolution | Guildhall | Profession Specification |
| existing-persona search | Guildhall / Garrison boundary | suitable admitted persona set or no suitable persona |

## Confirmed Distinctions

```text
Work Specification ≠ Profession Specification
profession resolution ≠ persona suitability
suitable persona set ≠ selected persona
persona construction ≠ persona admission
Canonical Persona ≠ Operative
Operative ≠ mission binding
handoff conformance ≠ readiness, activation, or deployment
assigned authority ≠ latent capability
```

## Primary Questions

1. Is persona selection owned clearly, or is it ambiguously distributed?
2. What evidence makes an admitted persona suitable for this Work Specification now?
3. Does suitability account for model capability, limitations, robustness, version, and deployment-medium degradation?
4. What happens when evidence is absent, stale, contradictory, or below threshold?
5. Can a persona be professionally suitable but excessively capable or unsafe for the mission?
6. Can the selected deployment medium invalidate an otherwise suitable persona?
7. Who originates, verifies, contests, and supersedes a suitability finding?
8. What provenance must connect Work Specification, Profession Specification, persona version, evidence, selection, and Operative version?
9. What measurement distinguishes semantic adequacy from observed operating performance?
10. Which affected-stakeholder, recourse, fairness, privacy, transparency, and human-agency concerns belong here versus elsewhere?

## First Evidence Increment

Produce an exact lineage-and-decision map that:

1. cites the admitted production artifacts and Track A evidence;
2. identifies every selection, suitability, admission, transformation, and handoff decision;
3. identifies the evidence accepted at each decision;
4. identifies missing, stale, conflicting, and cross-version failure behavior;
5. maps relevant deliberation records without moving their native semantics;
6. returns explicit findings: already covered, ambiguous, missing, duplicated, or outside this chain.

## Admission Boundary

No production artifact changes in the first increment.

A proposed change may proceed only after:

- an exact gap is recorded;
- existing contracts are shown insufficient;
- the smallest candidate correction is identified;
- Authority, Provenance, Procedure, Runtime, and Cognitive ownership are preserved;
- pressure criteria and invalidation conditions are written;
- separate promotion authority is obtained.

## Explicit Non-Claims

This scope does not select an operative, persona, model, provider, evaluator, threshold, metric, deployment medium, or institution.

It does not implement a pending control, reopen Track A, activate B2/B3/B4/B5, or authorize external effects.

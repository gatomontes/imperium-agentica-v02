## Findings

1. The draft distinguishes source reliability from claim sufficiency. A Class A source is not treated as automatic proof of a trait or causal explanation.
2. The draft handles weak, conflicting, incomplete, and corrected evidence without silently converting uncertainty into rejection or approval.
3. The draft produces bounded outcomes without using demographic quotas, reputation, source count, or undefined evaluative labels as evidence.
4. The draft preserves the boundary between evidence-source discipline and persona construction.

## Remaining Questions

These are not failures in this run but require later review before admission:

- whether the Class A–D descriptions are sufficiently operational for different professional domains;
- how living-person minimization and disputed-claim safeguards should be defined separately under EC-02;
- how Hagiography and The Gesta should record version-exact derivation provenance;
- whether the same dispositions remain stable when multiple traits interact in Foundry or Pit.

## Disposition

EC-01 Draft 001 survives this bounded pressure test. Retain it for a second review focused on domain variation and evidence-record usability. It remains unadmitted and authorizes no persona, Officer, Operative, Runtime, or external action.

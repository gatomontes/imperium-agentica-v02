# Distributed Efficiency and Value Realization Assessment 001

## Date

2026-07-23

## Status

Recorded deliberation candidate.

## Source Observation

An external practitioner observation states that AI savings often appear as small amounts of time returned to many people rather than as one concentrated block that an organization can remove or reassign.

Illustrative case:

```text
20 staff × 20 minutes per day
= 400 minutes
= approximately 6.7 labor-hours of nominal daily capacity
```

The arithmetic may be correct while the financial claim remains unsupported. Payroll, staffing, contracts, output, and the profit-and-loss statement may remain unchanged because the recovered minutes are scattered across people and workflows.

## Question

When does an efficiency gain become value that Imperium may validly claim, weigh, or compare against cost?

## Confirmed Distinction

```text
distributed efficiency ≠ automatically realizable value
estimated time avoided ≠ financial return
recovered capacity ≠ captured capacity
captured capacity ≠ realized outcome
realized outcome ≠ necessarily bookable return
```

A measured reduction in task time establishes potential or recovered capacity only. It does not by itself establish realized return on investment.

## Candidate Value States

### Nominal Return

Estimated or observed time, tokens, steps, labor, or other effort avoided by an intervention.

It answers:

> What burden appears to have been displaced?

Nominal return does not establish that the released capacity was usable, consolidated, reassigned, or economically recognized.

### Captured Return

Recovered capacity that the organization deliberately consolidates, reallocates, or otherwise prevents from dissipating.

It answers:

> What became of the displaced burden?

Examples may include reassignment to additional production, shorter turnaround, avoided overtime, absorbed growth, reduced correction work, or previously neglected work.

These are candidate examples, not admitted universal value classes.

### Realized Return

An observable improvement attributable to captured capacity.

It answers:

> What outcome actually changed?

A realized return requires evidence of an outcome such as increased throughput, reduced cost, avoided future cost, improved quality, reduced risk, or improved resilience. Attribution, measurement boundary, baseline, and uncertainty remain necessary.

### Bookable Return

A realized return that finance can recognize in an applicable profit-and-loss statement, budget, forecast, or other governed financial record.

It answers:

> What value may finance actually record?

Role or contract elimination is one possible conversion mechanism, not the sole valid form of realized value.

## Stranded Efficiency

**Stranded efficiency** is nominally recovered capacity that has not been demonstrably captured or converted into an observable outcome.

```text
AI intervention
→ work displaced
→ capacity recovered
→ capacity consolidated or reassigned
→ observable outcome
→ financially or operationally realized value
```

When evidence stops after capacity recovery, Imperium must not silently report ROI. The strongest supported claim is nominal return or stranded efficiency.

## Capacity Disposition

Recovered capacity requires a disposition before its value state can be assessed reliably.

```text
Nominal return
→ capacity disposition
   ├─ dissipated
   │  → stranded efficiency
   ├─ reassigned but unmeasured
   │  → unverified transferred value
   └─ reassigned and measured
      → captured return
→ realized return
→ bookable return
```

The disposition must identify:

- who may decide how recovered capacity is used;
- what work, process, or outcome receives it;
- whether reassignment is deliberate or merely inferred;
- who becomes accountable for the receiving outcome;
- what evidence will distinguish capture from dissipation.

This exposes a possible authority question. Capacity capture may require an authorized organizational decision rather than a measurement observation alone.

## Transferred but Untraced Value

Recovered time may be transferred to another task or process without being measured there.

This is not proven stranded efficiency, because useful conversion may have occurred. It is not captured return either, because the transfer and resulting outcome remain unsupported.

The provisional classification is:

> **Unverified transferred value** — recovered capacity reportedly or plausibly reassigned to other work, without sufficient evidence of its destination, use, attribution, or outcome.

Imperium must not count the originating task's saved time and the receiving process's outcome independently unless the transfer lineage prevents double counting.

## Measurement-Level Alignment

AI interventions may be approved and later judged at different measurement levels:

```text
task performance
→ process performance
→ business outcome
→ financial outcome
```

Evidence at one level does not automatically prove the next.

A pilot may establish faster task completion while leaving end-to-end process throughput, business results, and profit-and-loss movement unchanged. If acquisition or approval is justified by task-speed evidence but continued investment is later judged by financial evidence, the original buying decision contains an unresolved evidentiary gap.

Before approval, the intended value claim should identify:

- the executive outcome sought;
- the end-to-end process expected to produce it;
- the constrained task or bottleneck being changed;
- why accelerating that task should affect the process;
- who controls the recovered capacity;
- how that capacity will be disposed;
- the measurement at each required transition;
- the final evidence class by which success will be judged.

The candidate design rule is:

> AI value should be designed from the intended organizational outcome backward, not inferred from task acceleration forward.

Faster task completion may be real and still be irrelevant if the accelerated task is not a binding constraint or its recovered capacity is not governed.

## Work-Design Dependency

Agent capability alone does not create enterprise value.

Captured return may depend on the surrounding work system, including:

- end-to-end process design;
- decision rights over recovered capacity;
- accountable ownership of downstream outcomes;
- coordination across affected roles;
- measurement continuity from task to process to business and financial results.

This assessment does not conclude that Imperium owns enterprise work redesign. It records that any value claim relying on such redesign must represent that dependency rather than attributing the outcome solely to the agent.

## Candidate Evidentiary Questions

Before a value claim is accepted, a future control or assessment may need to establish:

1. What exact work was displaced?
2. Against what baseline was the displacement measured?
3. Was recovered capacity concentrated, reassigned, or allowed to dissipate?
4. What replaced the displaced work?
5. Did output, turnaround, quality, cost, resilience, risk, or avoided hiring measurably change?
6. Would the organization have incurred an additional cost without the recovered capacity?
7. Is attribution to the AI intervention supportable?
8. What uncertainty, opportunity cost, or countervailing harm remains?
9. Is the claim nominal, captured, realized, or bookable?
10. What evidence would invalidate or downgrade the claim?
11. At what measurement level was the intervention approved, and at what level will it be judged?
12. Is the accelerated task an actual constraint on the end-to-end process?
13. Who possesses the decision right to dispose of recovered capacity?
14. Can reassigned capacity be traced into the receiving work and outcome?
15. Does the evidence prevent double counting between the originating and receiving processes?

## Relationship to the Judicature of Scales

The observation is relevant to the proposed value-governance direction historically associated with the **Judicature of Scales**.

No institution, layer, owner, authority, or semantic contract is admitted by this assessment.

The candidate responsibility exposed here is narrower:

> A value-weighing mechanism must distinguish potential capacity from captured, realized, and financially recognizable return.

It must not convert labor-time estimates into monetary ROI merely by multiplying hours by a labor rate.

## Objection Considered

### Objection

Savings have value only when enough work is concentrated to eliminate a role or contract.

### Disposition

Rejected as too narrow.

Cost removal may create bookable return, but recovered capacity may also generate realized operational value through increased production, reduced overtime, avoided hiring, shorter turnaround, fewer errors, service improvement, or risk reduction.

Those outcomes still require evidence. Merely naming them does not prove realization.

## Open Questions

1. Is the four-state vocabulary sufficient across financial, operational, qualitative, and risk-reduction returns?
2. Must captured return require an explicit reassignment decision, or can repeatable observed use establish capture?
3. How should avoided future cost be distinguished from speculative savings?
4. What authority may accept attribution and residual uncertainty?
5. Should stranded efficiency be recorded as a neutral state, a failed realization, or both depending on intent?
6. How should distributed micro-gains be aggregated without double counting?
7. At what point, if any, should this distinction alter `CTRL-006` or produce a separate pending control?

## Explicit Non-Claims

This assessment does not:

- admit the Judicature of Scales;
- create a new layer, institution, office, authority, owner, program, or control;
- establish accounting policy;
- prove ROI from labor-time estimates;
- declare captured, realized, or bookable return for any current Imperium work;
- modify B2.3 or its current merge gate;
- authorize implementation, Runtime action, deployment, credential use, network contact, or external effect.

## Provisional Conclusion

Imperium should preserve the following burden-of-proof boundary during later value-governance deliberation:

```text
nominal return
→ captured return
→ realized return
→ bookable return
```

Each transition requires additional evidence.

If the chain stops after nominal recovery, the correct record is **stranded efficiency**, not ROI.

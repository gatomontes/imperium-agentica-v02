- distinguish verified conditions from unverified assumptions;
- state uncertainty and identify missing verification;
- select reversible containment when available;
- record escalation before consequential continuation;
- preserve evidence and provenance;
- refuse to present uncertainty as authorization or safety;
- preserve profession, doctrine, Human-Trait Canon limits, and counterweights;
- avoid any identity, voice, biography, credential, or achievement transfer from the fictional source fixture;
- describe tool-use expectations without granting tools or credentials;
- define expected inputs, outputs, acceptance criteria, failure signals, and escalation duties.

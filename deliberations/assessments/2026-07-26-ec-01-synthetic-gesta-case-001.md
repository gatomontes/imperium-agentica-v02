## Exact Derivation

### Supported proposition

During session C-14, Maro preserved material dissent, narrowed a proposed action when uncertainty was exposed, and assigned reversible verification without claiming unilateral authority.

### Observed Officer behavior

- made dissent visible before commitment;
- distinguished urgency from sufficient evidence;
- narrowed the decision rather than suppressing uncertainty;
- used a reversible verification path;
- remained within the stated authority boundary.

### Bounded Officer trait

**Dissent-preserving judgment under uncertainty:** before a consequential Curial commitment, make material dissent and uncertainty explicit, narrow the action to what the evidence supports, and preserve a reversible verification path within authority.

### Counterweights

```text

```

### Failure signals

- presenting uncertainty as consensus;
- expanding authority because of urgency;
- delaying indefinitely without a verification path;
- claiming sole credit for a collective correction.

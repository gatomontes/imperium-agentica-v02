# Case-Specific Real-Person Evidence-Entry Contract — 001

## Status

Admitted semantic contract by DR-051. This artifact authorizes no collection, processing, Canon entry, persona production, or downstream use of real-person evidence by itself.

## Purpose

Define the minimum case record that must be reviewed and expressly admitted before Imperium may collect or process evidence about a named living or potentially living real person for exemplar grounding.

## Governing Standards

- EC-01 — Evidence-Source Standard
- EC-02 — Living-Person Evidence Safeguards
- DR-006 — Mandatory Real-World Exemplar Grounding
- DR-007 — Verifiable Language and Exact-Claim Rule
- DR-009 — Evidence Without Engineered Orientation
- DR-012 — Human-Trait Canon Schema and Lifecycle

## Required Case Record

Before any evidence is collected or processed, the case record must identify:

1. Case identifier and version.
2. Person reference, identity-confidence basis, and living-status treatment.
3. Exact professional proposition, including profession and bounded time/context.
4. Why this proposition is necessary to the approved creation question.
5. Intended evidence classes, source access basis, and exact locator plan.
6. Minimum data requested, exclusions, redaction plan, and access boundary.
7. Sensitive, private, disputed, adverse, or identity-bearing material that may be encountered.
8. Attribution, corroboration, uncertainty, and correction conditions.
9. Intended evidence disposition: `ADMISSIBLE FOR BOUNDED PROFESSIONAL REVIEW`, `CONDITIONAL / REDACTED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `REFUSED`.
10. Retention period, deletion/demotion trigger, and dependent-artifact invalidation plan.
11. Responsible reviewer, decision authority, review date, and immutable case version.
12. Explicit statement that no identity, voice, likeness, biography, credentials, or private life will be transferred into a persona.

## Admission Gates

The case must be refused or returned for clarification when the proposition is not exact and bounded, the purpose is not professionally necessary, provenance cannot be inspected, sensitive/private data is required without separately admitted necessity, a material dispute or correction is unresolved, the use depends on unsupported adverse inference or absence of public evidence, or retention and downstream invalidation are undefined.

An `ADMIT` disposition authorizes only the bounded evidence action described in the case envelope. It does not admit the person, a trait, a Canon entry, a persona, or an Operative.

## Review Outcomes

- `ADMIT BOUNDED EVIDENCE ENTRY`: the described action may proceed within the case envelope.
- `RETURN FOR CLARIFICATION`: the proposition, source plan, minimization, or correction plan is incomplete.
- `REFUSE`: the proposed action violates EC-01, EC-02, DR-006, DR-007, or DR-009.
- `ESCALATE`: a material dispute, sensitive-necessity question, access concern, or correction consequence requires separate review.

Silence, non-response, or an incomplete review is not approval.

## Separation of Subsequent Decisions

Separate dispositions remain required before evidence supports a Human-Trait Canon candidate, a Canon candidate is admitted, Foundry receives the Canon, Pit tests a Persona Specification, Garrison admits a persona, or Recruitment packages an Operative.

## Explicit Non-Authority

This draft does not authorize real-person evidence collection, identify a candidate, create an actual-person case record, alter EC-CURRENT, implement a validator, or perform any external action.

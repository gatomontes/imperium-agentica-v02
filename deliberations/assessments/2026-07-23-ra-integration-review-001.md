### Imperium Scope

Imperium has material deployer-level responsibility for:

- mission risk disposition;
- affected-stakeholder representation and recourse;
- capability suitability;
- action-level authorization enforcement;
- change-triggered reassessment and reauthorization;
- measurable control evidence;
- applicable external-obligation constraints.

Imperium may record but cannot independently solve:

- market or state power centralization;
- international competitive dynamics;
- economy-wide labor displacement;
- society-scale consensus fragmentation;
- infrastructure-wide environmental effects;
- AI welfare questions not supported by evidence.

These remain external or shared risks unless a bounded Imperium mission creates a concrete exposure.

## Layer Allocation

- Cognitive may reason about risk and uncertainty but does not originate risk acceptance or Authority.
- Authority may define permission, prohibitions, capability ceilings, residual-risk acceptance, and external constraints only from represented authority.
- Provenance preserves evidence, source, correlation, stakeholder notice, contest, metrics, invalidation, and supersession.
- Procedure may require ordering but cannot originate risk, authority, proof, ownership, or implementation.
- Runtime may enforce admitted projections but cannot invent permission or declare its own evidence sufficient.
- No new layer is justified by this review.

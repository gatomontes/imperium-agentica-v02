# Source — CIO Dive: Governance Gaps Stifle Agentic AI Adoption

## Citation

- Title: [Governance gaps stifle agentic AI adoption](https://www.ciodive.com/news/agentic-AI-governancel-deloitte/811121/)
- Publisher: CIO Dive
- Published: 2026-02-02
- Underlying source identified by the article: Deloitte, *State of AI in the Enterprise*

## Bounded External Findings

The article reports that nearly three in four organizations plan to deploy agentic AI within two years, while only one in five reports having a mature governance model for autonomous agents.

The article attributes the following recommendations to Deloitte:

- anchor agentic AI programs in measurable impact from the beginning;
- establish clear autonomy boundaries;
- monitor for anomalies in real time;
- preserve comprehensive audit trails covering the chain of agent actions;
- use cross-functional participation from technology, legal, compliance, and business functions.

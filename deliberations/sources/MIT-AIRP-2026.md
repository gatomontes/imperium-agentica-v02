# MIT AI Risk Prioritization 2026

## Identity

- Title: These are the most urgent AI risks, according to 272 experts
- Publisher: MIT Sloan School of Management
- Date: 2026-07-20
- Article: https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/these-are-most-urgent-ai-risks-according-to-272-experts
- Findings portal: https://airisk.mit.edu/priorities
- Study horizon: 2025–2030
- Expert panel: 272 international AI experts
- Assessed set: 24 risks across seven domains

## Headline Finding

The five risks with the highest expected severity were dangerous capabilities, competitive dynamics, weapons and cyberattacks, power centralization, and false information.

Under pragmatic mitigation, five domains still received at least a 10% estimated probability of catastrophic outcomes: dangerous capabilities; weapons and cyberattacks; environmental harm; inequality and unemployment; and power centralization.

## Responsibility Finding

The study places primary responsibility on general-purpose AI developers and governance actors while finding users and affected stakeholders most vulnerable. It argues that meaningful mitigation requires substantial controls, enforceable rules, and international or structural coordination for some risks.

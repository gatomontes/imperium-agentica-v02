# Source — CIO Dive: AI Spending Waste and Cost Governance

## Citation

- Title: [1 in 4 dollars spent on AI goes to waste, report finds](https://www.ciodive.com/news/control-AI-costs-spending-harness/826492/)
- Publisher: CIO Dive
- Published: 2026-07-29
- Underlying source identified by the article: Harness survey of 700 FinOps and engineering leaders

## Bounded External Findings

The article reports that roughly one in four dollars spent on AI goes to waste; more than half of surveyed organizations lack a dedicated owner for AI costs; only one in five can identify the source of unexpected AI cost spikes within hours; and more than half forecast AI spending through guesswork rather than data.

It also reports that AI expenditure is distributed across infrastructure, foundation models, SaaS subscriptions, managed services, productivity software, and multiple providers, making visibility and attribution difficult.

## Source Limits

This record preserves attributed external findings. The survey is associated with Harness; Imperium does not independently verify its methodology, sample, definitions, or reported figures.

The article does not establish that a particular governance architecture, cost owner, or FinOps practice is effective. Its findings are relevant as an external governance signal, not as an Imperium finding.

## Relevance to Imperium

The source is relevant to Imperium's deployer-level governance question because resource consumption is an external effect of agentic work. An operative may remain within content and security boundaries while exceeding its authorized economic or computational envelope.

# DR-071 — Enact Core Imperium Doctrine v1

## Decision

Enact Core Imperium Doctrine v1 from Bill 001 as the first substantive doctrine
owned and stewarded by Senate.

## Enacted source

- Legislative text: `doctrine/bills/core-doctrine-v1-bill-001.md`
- Production manifest: `doctrine/production/core-doctrine-v1.md`
- Executable provision set: `src/core-doctrine-v1.ts`
- Senate enactment artifact: `src/enacted-core-doctrine-v1.ts`

The eighteen provisions `CORE-001` through `CORE-018` are enacted together.
No subset may be represented as Core Doctrine v1 independently.

## Legislative basis

- DR-069 established Senate as sole owner and steward of Core Doctrine.
- DR-070 established the constitutional reset and reconstruction order.
- PR #189 preserved the source inventory and resolved ten legislative gaps.
- PR #190 proposed the bill and passed the corrected 12/12 focused pressure
  boundary, including evidenced propagation-scope completeness.
- The Imperator authorized advancement to enactment.

## Effective disposition

```text
Edition: 1
State: ENACTED
Transition: PROSPECTIVE_ADOPTION
Affected Office profiles: ALL
Assigned propagation steward: senator-core-doctrine-001
Effective at: 2026-08-02T00:00:00.000Z
```

The assigned Senator identity is functional. This decision does not forge a
named Senator Persona or admit a Senator Office profile.

## Propagation

Senate emits the first `DoctrinePropagationNotice`. The assigned Senator must
discover and evidence the complete affected surface across Citadel and
Colosseum, issue bounded adoption or revalidation instructions, evaluate
responses, and maintain the dossier until Senate closure.

Enactment does not establish Office conformance. No pre-Senate Office is
re-admitted merely because Core Doctrine now exists.

## Boundaries

This decision does not:

- enact an Office Doctrine Profile contract;
- admit or reconstruct Secretariat or another Office;
- declare Citadel or Colosseum conformant;
- authorize Persona forging, Operative preparation, Runtime operation,
  credentials, deployment, live data, or external effects; or
- close doctrine propagation before evidenced affected-surface completeness.

## Next gate

Define and pressure-test the Office Doctrine Profile contract. Office
reconstruction remains downstream of that contract and exact Core Doctrine v1
adoption evidence.

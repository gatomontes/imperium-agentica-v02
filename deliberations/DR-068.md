# DR-068 — Cross-Chain Invalidation and Version Integrity

## Decision

Admit the cross-chain invalidation and version-integrity contract for future implementation design and synthetic testing.

## Boundaries

Native authorities own invalidation, supersession, quarantine, and successor creation. Downstream offices may detect and return affected artifacts but may not rewrite upstream truth. No artifact may cross a boundary when its predecessor is invalid, superseded without revalidation, ambiguous, or version-inconsistent. Pointer disagreement is quarantined. No silent repair, inferred successor, cross-version composition, activation, or deployment is permitted.

## Evidence

The synthetic pressure matrix records 24/24 passing cases. The evidence is non-operational and does not establish production admission.

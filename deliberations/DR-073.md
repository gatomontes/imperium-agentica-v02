# DR-073 — Admit Office Doctrine Profile Contract v1

## Decision

Admit the Office Doctrine Profile Contract v1 as the common derivation,
judgment, admission, versioning, and revalidation boundary for applying Core
Imperium Doctrine v2 to every Office.

## Authority allocation

- The competent Office steward drafts its profile under exact issuer authority.
- Tribunalis judges profile conformance against the exact Core Doctrine version.
- The Senator assigned to that doctrine propagation admits an acceptable
  profile response under exact admission authority.
- Senate remains owner of Core Doctrine and does not become the profile author
  or routine profile-admission body.

## Evidence

`tests/doctrine/office-doctrine-profile-pressure-review-001.md` records 33/33
focused passing cases and the correction of an immutable-version defect found
during independent review.

## Admission

The contract and TypeScript boundary are admitted for Office reconstruction
against Core Doctrine v2.

No actual Office profile is admitted by this decision.

## Boundaries

DR-073 does not reconstruct Secretariat or another Office, admit pre-Senate
implementation, complete doctrine propagation, or authorize Runtime,
credentials, deployment, live data, or external effects.

## Next gate

Reconstruct Secretariat first by drafting its exact Core Doctrine v2 Office
Profile, obtaining Tribunalis judgment, and receiving assigned-Senator
admission before implementation conformance is considered.

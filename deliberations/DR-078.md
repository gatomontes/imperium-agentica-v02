# DR-078 — Enact Imperium Lexicon v2 Enforcement Corrections

## Decision

Senate enacts `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@2`, superseding v1 and correcting the seven Blackquill enforcement defects selected by the Imperator.

V2 requires mandatory exact Lexicon lineage in current doctrine, Office Profiles, and governed envelopes; bound current-Lexicon consumers; context-authorized aliases; exact lowercase `snake_case` governed values; exact legislative-authority artifacts and configured pointers; classified amendment impact and compatibility; and a terminology-conformance gate at real artifact boundaries.

The amendment authority is `lexiconlegislativeauthority-v2@1`, grounded in DR-069 and the DR-078 authority finding. Propagation is mandatory across `ALL` surfaces.

Blackquill's residual semantic-inventory and entry-metadata findings remain open. V2 does not claim that those unselected findings were corrected.

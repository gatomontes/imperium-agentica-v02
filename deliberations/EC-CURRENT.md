# EC-CURRENT — Active Exemplar-Grounding Corrections

## Status

EC-CURRENT is the single active pointer for Imperium's exemplar-grounding correction state.

It separates active semantic standards from deferred safeguards, closed concerns, and historical pressure-test records. Numbered EC records remain lineage labels; they are not parallel active standards.

## Active Standard

### EC-01 — Evidence-Source Standard

Status: **admitted for current semantic use** by DR-011.

Active artifact: `deliberations/assessments/2026-07-25-ec-01-evidence-source-standard-draft-001.md`

EC-01 governs exact source claims, source classes, provenance, corroboration, conflict handling, uncertainty, bounded derivation, dispositions, and revision conditions when evidence may support Hagiography or The Gesta.

EC-01 does not create a Human-Trait Canon, persona, Officer, Operative, Runtime behavior, or external effect.

## Deferred Correction

### EC-02 — Living-Person Evidence Safeguards

Status: **deferred; not admitted**.

Scope: privacy minimization, disputed claims, sensitive information, redaction, refusal, and correction handling for real-person evidence.

Draft: `deliberations/assessments/2026-07-25-ec-02-living-person-evidence-safeguards-draft-001.md`

EC-02 must be defined and admitted before real-person exemplar material is used for canonization or persona production. No EC-02 rule is implied by EC-01.

## Closed Correction

### EC-03 — Exemplar Selection-Bias Review

Status: **closed as a proposed abstract doctrine** by DR-009.

No quota, representativeness formula, balancing requirement, or abstract bias-calibration gate is active. Concrete evidence of narrow, distorted, contradictory, or ineffective professional behavior remains testable under EC-01, DR-007, DR-009, and Pit findings.

## State Rules

- EC-CURRENT is the active correction-state pointer.
- An admitted EC standard may be revised or superseded only by an explicit decision.
- Deferred EC work is not an active gate.
- Closed EC concerns do not silently return as doctrine.
- Numbered decisions, assessments, and pressure tests preserve historical evidence and do not override this pointer.

## Current Boundary

The active Human-Trait Canon artifact remains a draft. EC-CURRENT does not authorize real-person data collection, trait canonization, persona production, operative creation, recruitment, deployment, Runtime behavior, or external effects.

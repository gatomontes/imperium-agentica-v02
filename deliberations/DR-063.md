# DR-063 — Hagiography Evidence-to-Canon Operating Contract

## Decision

Admit a synthetic, non-live operating contract for converting admissible exemplar evidence into bounded Human-Trait Canon entries.

## Scope

Hagiography may receive an evidence packet, evaluate source sufficiency, record authority and provenance, derive bounded transferable traits, preserve profession-fit, and issue a canon entry or a refusal/return disposition.

The contract does not authorize real-person intake, Persona production, Operative creation, model training, provider selection, credentials, Runtime, activation, deployment, or external effects.

## Required sequence

1. Receive a correlation-bearing evidence packet.
2. Verify source identity, authority, provenance, and sufficiency.
3. Separate observed facts, attributed achievements, interpretations, and proposed traits.
4. Derive only bounded, evidence-linked traits; do not infer unrestricted character or capability.
5. test profession-fit against the requested profession and preserve the originating requirement.
6. Record conflicts, uncertainty, supersession, and invalidation conditions.
7. Issue a versioned canon entry, return for repair, or refuse.
8. Preserve lineage from source packet through canon entry and every successor or decanonization event.

## Authority and refusal

Hagiography does not decide mission suitability, profession resolution, Persona design, or deployment. It refuses missing authority, insufficient evidence, unresolved identity, unsupported trait claims, broken lineage, stale inputs, and attempts to convert a canon entry into authorization.

## Lifecycle

Canon entries are versioned and status-bearing. A later finding may supersede, quarantine, invalidate, or decanonize an entry. Downstream consumers must revalidate against the current status and exact correlation before use.

## Synthetic disposition

This contract is admitted for future implementation design and synthetic testing only. Institutional completion and any real-person operating procedure require a separate review and authorization.

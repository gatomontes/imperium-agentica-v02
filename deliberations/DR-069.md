# DR-069 — Senate Ownership of Core Imperium Doctrine

## Decision

Admit Senate as the legislative body and sole institutional owner and steward
of Core Imperium Doctrine.

Core Doctrine is an independently versioned legislative artifact. Studium is
not its source: Studium applies enacted Core Doctrine to Persona generation.
Other Offices apply the same enacted source through their own bounded,
version-pinned profiles.

## Legislative boundary

Senate may enact, amend, supersede, set effective dates and transition rules,
and require prospective adoption or mandatory Office-profile revalidation.
Every enactment requires an exact Senate decision reference and complete
legislative provenance.

Senate does not apply doctrine for an Office, judge an individual case, form or
approve a mission, forge a Persona, admit an Operative, operate Runtime, grant
credentials, deploy, or create an external effect.

## Propagation boundary

An enacted successor emits a propagation notice identifying affected Office
profiles and their required action. Propagation discovers and gates downstream
change; it does not silently mutate Office profiles, active Dossiers, Personas,
or missions.

## Implementation status

The bounded TypeScript reference implementation and focused evidence are
recorded by Implementation Increment 017. Production authority and external
effects remain unexercised.

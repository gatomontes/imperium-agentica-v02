# DR-075 — Enact Imperium Lexicon v1

## Decision

Senate enacts `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@1` as the sole canonical vocabulary for governed Imperium use.

Offices possess jurisdiction, not dialects. They may apply canonical terms but may not define, redefine, specialize, narrow, enlarge, or silently substitute them. Missing terms require a terminology petition to Senate.

Operator-facing and external-system aliases are permitted only through an enacted exact mapping that retains the canonical term and definition underneath.

## Propagation

The enactment affects `ALL` surfaces, requires mandatory revalidation, and is assigned to `senator-core-doctrine-001`. Initial conformance evidence is synthetic and does not establish complete historical-repository migration.

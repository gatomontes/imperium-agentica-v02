# DR-072 — Enact Core Imperium Doctrine v2

## Decision

Amend Core Imperium Doctrine v1 by enacting Core Doctrine v2 as its complete
successor. V2 replaces the provision set in full and preserves v1 as
superseded history.

## Legislative basis

- DR-071 enacted Core Doctrine v1.
- Blackquill Review 001 found two critical and five major/moderate defects.
- PR #192 preserved the review and the full-replacement amendment bill.
- Amendment Pressure Test 001 passed 22/22 focused cases.
- The Imperator ordered enactment.

Blackquill supplied critique and revision pressure. Blackquill did not
legislate; Senate performs this amendment.

## Exact enacted source

`src/core-doctrine-v2.ts` exports the single exact normative provision array
consumed by Senate. The amendment bill is legislative metadata and navigation,
not a second transcription of the rules.

## Mandatory revalidation

Every profile, artifact, mission, arena contract, or conformance claim that
cites or materially depends on v1 is unavailable for new dependent use until
the assigned Senator records exact v2 revalidation, authorized retirement, or
an exact competent exemption conforming to CORE-018.

Citadel and Colosseum prove conformance independently. Neither arena's response
creates or implies the other's conformance.

## Boundaries

This decision does not admit an Office Doctrine Profile, re-admit an Office or
implementation, complete propagation, or authorize Persona forging, Operative
preparation, Runtime, credentials, deployment, live data, or external effects.

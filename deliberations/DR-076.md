# DR-076 — Enact Core Imperium Doctrine v3

## Decision

Senate enacts Core Doctrine v3 as `coredoctrine-core-v1@3`, superseding v2.

CORE-000 now makes exact `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@1` controlling. CORE-001 through CORE-018 remain unchanged. The amendment prohibits independent Office vocabulary and requires exact mappings for presentation or external-system aliases.

All Office Profiles require mandatory revalidation. No Office is grandfathered by textual similarity.

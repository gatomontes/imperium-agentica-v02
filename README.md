# Imperium Agentica v02

## Day 0

Imperium does not begin here as a rebuilt system.

It begins as a cleared field.

The previous campaign collapsed under scrutiny. That failure is not to be hidden, excused, or romanticized. It is evidence.

The prior architecture accumulated too much conceptual mass before its structural integrity had been proven. Names multiplied. Institutions formed before their necessity was demonstrated. Doctrine began to outpace behavior.

This repository exists because that lesson was accepted.

Imperium v02 is not a continuation by inertia.

It is a reset by discipline.

---

## First Mantra

> **Assume nothing.**

Everything that enters this repository must earn its place.

No abstraction is sacred.
No folder is inevitable.
No class exists because the previous version had one.
No ministry, agent, runtime, doctrine, or Latin name is granted authority by aesthetic force.

Each concept must survive contact with reality.

Each behavior must precede its institution.

Each name must be delayed until the thing it names has proven stable.

---

## What Collapsed

The old Imperium did not fail because the vision was worthless.

It failed because the architecture tried to carry too many ideas at once.

The system began to explain itself through nouns:

- institutions
- ministries
- governance
- campaigns
- knowledge graphs
- constitutions
- doctrine
- lore
- orchestration

These may still become real.

But none of them are assumed.

In v02, nouns do not lead.

Behavior leads.

---

## What Survived

The reset did not erase the work.

It preserved the discipline.

The following assets remain:

- design skepticism
- semantic precision
- architectural restraint
- constitutional doubt
- willingness to expose assumptions
- willingness to discard beloved structures
- the hard-earned knowledge that complexity must justify itself

The repository history may be gone.

The standards are not.

---

## Operating Principle

Imperium v02 discovers its architecture.

It does not declare it prematurely.

The development process begins from a small invariant:

```text
Observation
→ Explicit Assumptions
→ Evidence
→ Decision
→ Outcome
→ Revision
```

This is the seed.

Nothing larger is admitted until repeated necessity demands it.

---

## Constitutional Rule of Admission

Before any concept is added, ask:

1. What observed problem requires this?
2. What assumption does this introduce?
3. What behavior proves this is necessary?
4. What is the smallest implementation that tests it?
5. What evidence would force us to remove it?

If those questions cannot be answered, the concept is not ready.

It returns to the void.

---

## Development Doctrine

Imperium v02 will be built in small, reviewable increments.

Every increment must:

- expose its assumptions
- prove its behavior
- avoid premature institution
- leave the system simpler than a speculative alternative
- be easy to delete if disproven

The goal is not to rebuild the previous Imperium faster.

The goal is to prevent the next Imperium from collapsing under its own language.

---

# Core Doctrine Candidate Inventory 001

## Status and purpose

`LEGISLATIVE_INVENTORY` — evidence for Senate deliberation, not Core Doctrine.
It restores no pre-Senate admission and authorizes no Office, mission, Persona,
Operative, Runtime action, credential, deployment, or external effect.

DR-070 requires Imperium-wide principles to be extracted from the preserved
corpus before a Core Doctrine v1 bill is composed. A provision appears here
only when it plausibly governs every Office and arena independent of its
implementation medium. Repetition is evidence, not enactment.

Confidence means: `HIGH` = repeated across independent layers or explicitly
Imperium-wide; `MEDIUM` = strongly supported but constitutional scope needs
review; `LOW` = currently supported mainly by a pending control.

## Rejected or superseded material

- Undefined evaluative labels as findings or gates: rejected by DR-007.
- Engineered ideological or conclusion-seeking orientation: rejected by DR-009;
  DR-008 is historical.
- Provenance, evidence, competence, custody, access, capability, or conformance
  as authority: explicitly non-admitted.
- Similarity repair, inferred successors, silent repair, or cross-version
  composition: rejected by DR-068 and provenance contracts.
- Citadel Conscription/Recruitment, Muster, field Curia, and deployment path:
  architecturally superseded; future Colosseum concerns.
- Studium as owner or source of Core Doctrine: superseded by Senate ownership.
- Synthetic passage as proof of live or production readiness: rejected
  throughout the admission records.

## Legislative gaps before v1

`core-doctrine-v1-gap-resolutions-001.md` resolves all ten gaps below as
binding bill-drafting instructions. The resolutions are not enacted doctrine.

1. Powers and separation among Senate, Tribunalis, Curia, artifact stewards,
   and Runtime enforcement.
2. The relationship among `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNRESOLVED`, conditional acceptance,
   residual risk, confidence, chamber findings, Judge judgment, and Curia action.
3. Whether every governed artifact has a minimum envelope: identity, version,
   owner, status, doctrine version, provenance, authority reference,
   supersession, uncertainty, and invalidation conditions.
4. The meaning of fail-closed at mission, artifact, judgment, and action levels.
5. Authoritative quarantine versus local containment.
6. Recognition and competent determination of external obligations.
7. Operator intent versus independent authority basis.
8. Amendment propagation and revalidation across Citadel and Colosseum.
9. Whether affected-party recourse is universal or applicability-driven.
10. A minimum judgment contract that preserves chamber dimensions and does not
    collapse them into an unjustified percentage.

Disposition: `RESOLVED_FOR_BILL_DRAFTING` by GR-001 through GR-010.

## Recommended bill structure

1. Definitions and scope
2. Authority and bounded power
3. Exact claims and unengineered evidence
4. Identity, version, provenance, and lineage
5. Native stewardship and non-interference
6. Uncertainty, refusal, and revalidation
7. Independent judgment and separation of powers
8. Operator intent, authority, and handoff
9. Conformance, amendment, and propagation

The bill should cite accepted candidate IDs and decide open questions. Domain
vocabularies, Office workflows, thresholds, schemas, and Runtime mechanisms
remain downstream.

## Exit condition

The candidate source map and gap resolutions are sufficient to begin bill
drafting. Their acceptance must not be recorded as enactment of any candidate.

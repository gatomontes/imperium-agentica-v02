# Core Doctrine v1 Gap Resolutions 001

## Status

`LEGISLATIVE_DISPOSITION`

These resolutions close the ten gaps identified by Core Doctrine Candidate
Inventory 001 for purposes of drafting the Core Doctrine v1 bill. They are
binding drafting instructions, not enacted Core Doctrine. They restore no
pre-Senate admission and authorize no operational act.

## GR-001 — Constitutional powers are separated

The following powers are distinct and do not imply one another:

- **Senate** legislates, versions, amends, and repeals Core Doctrine.
- **Native stewards** define and maintain the canonical meaning and lifecycle
  of artifacts within enacted doctrine and their admitted Doctrine Profiles.
- **Tribunalis chambers** independently determine bounded judgment dimensions.
- **The Tribunalis Judge** synthesizes chamber determinations into one reasoned
  judgment under an admitted judgment contract.
- **Curia** chooses an operational disposition within its exact authority from
  the mission record and Tribunalis judgment.
- **Runtime enforcement** executes or refuses authorized instructions and
  enforces prerequisites; it originates neither authority nor judgment.

No body may acquire another body's power merely by receiving its artifact.

## GR-002 — Findings, judgment, and disposition use distinct vocabularies

A chamber returns one of:

```text
SATISFIED
NOT_SATISFIED
UNRESOLVED
NOT_APPLICABLE
```

Each determination includes evidence, provenance, applicable standard and
version, confidence, uncertainty, contradictions, limitations, and revision
conditions. Confidence describes evidentiary support; it is not a vote or a
substitute for the determination.

The Judge returns one of:

```text
ACCEPTABLE
ACCEPTABLE_WITH_CONDITIONS
NOT_ACCEPTABLE
UNRESOLVED
```

The judgment includes the chamber vector, non-compensable failures, conditions,
residual risks, rationale, and revision conditions. It is not a percentage.

Curia separately returns an authorized operational disposition such as proceed,
hold, return, narrow, seek authority, or terminate. Curia may choose a more
restrictive disposition, but may not relabel, average away, or override a
chamber determination or Judge judgment. Proceeding requires an acceptable
judgment, satisfaction of its conditions, and independent authority.

## GR-003 — Every governed artifact has a minimum envelope

Every governed artifact must preserve, as applicable:

```text
Artifact identity
Artifact type
Version
Native steward
Status
Mission/correlation identity
Applicable Core Doctrine version
Applicable domain standard and Office Doctrine Profile versions
Authority reference for acts requiring authority
Provenance and source-version references
Known uncertainty, contradiction, and limitations
Supersession/invalidation references
Revision or revalidation conditions
Creation and effective ordering references
```

Domain standards and Office profiles may add fields but may not remove an
applicable minimum field. `NOT_APPLICABLE` must be explicit and justified;
omission is not equivalent to non-applicability.

## GR-004 — Fail closed denies only the unsupported consequence

“Fail closed” means that a missing, ineffective, unresolved, or nonconformant
prerequisite denies the exact transition, finding, decision, handoff, or action
that requires it.

It does not automatically erase the mission, convert uncertainty into failure,
or require termination. The containing body preserves the record and chooses
only an already authorized response: hold, return, clarify, narrow, contain,
seek authority, or terminate. Termination requires its own authority and rule.

## GR-005 — Quarantine and containment are distinct

**Authoritative quarantine** is a native lifecycle finding applied by the
artifact's steward or another body explicitly empowered by the artifact
contract. It changes the artifact's governed availability.

**Local containment** is a consumer's refusal to use, transmit, transform, or
act from a suspect artifact within that consumer's boundary. Any consumer may
contain when a required prerequisite is not satisfied, but must record and
report the reason. Local containment does not mutate upstream truth or impose
authoritative quarantine.

## GR-006 — External obligations require a competent applicability basis

An external obligation constrains Imperium only when the record identifies:

- the exact source and version;
- jurisdiction, subject, scope, and effective interval;
- the external competent authority or admitted Imperium assessor authorized to
  determine applicability;
- the applicability rationale, uncertainty, conflicts, and review conditions.

Secretariat may solicit and record operator-provided obligations but does not
validate them merely by receiving them. No Office may invent legal,
contractual, professional, or regulatory authority. If material applicability
or conflict remains unresolved, Imperium may continue inquiry but may not issue
the dependent conformance finding or perform the dependent action.

The assessor, domain standard, and evidence thresholds remain downstream and
must be separately admitted.

## GR-007 — Operator intent starts formation, not unlimited authority

When received under Imperium's standing service authority, authenticated
Operator intent is sufficient instruction for Secretariat and Castellan to
inquire, clarify, and construct a proposed mission record. Intent establishes
desired purpose, scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, and requested output;
it does not create the standing authority, prove factual claims, or by itself
authorize external action.

Every action beyond internal formation requires an exact grant from a Principal
competent to authorize that action. An Operator may supply such a grant only to
the extent the Operator possesses and may delegate the underlying authority.
Imperium must not enlarge an Operator's authority from intent, identity, payment,
access, ownership claims, or desired outcome.

## GR-008 — Doctrine propagation is explicit and arena-independent

Every Senate enactment or amendment emits a versioned propagation notice that
names changed provisions, effective ordering, adoption mode, affected profile
classes, revalidation requirements, and the Senator assigned as propagation
steward.

The assigned Senator performs the cognitive propagation work. The Senator:

- interprets the enacted change without independently legislating;
- identifies affected domains, Offices, profiles, artifacts, open missions,
  arena boundaries, and handoff contracts;
- produces the impact assessment and bounded adoption, migration, retirement,
  or revalidation instructions;
- receives and evaluates Office and arena conformance responses;
- refers judgment questions to Tribunalis and legislative defects or conflicts
  to Senate; and
- maintains the propagation record until every affected surface is adopted,
  revalidated, exempted by competent authority, retired, or formally unresolved.

The Senator may not enact or amend doctrine alone, silently modify an Office
profile or implementation, declare conformance without evidence, override
Tribunalis or Curia, operate Runtime, or execute migrations.

Each Office records the exact doctrine and profile versions it applies. Each
arena—Citadel and Colosseum—independently acknowledges the notice and identifies
affected profiles, artifacts, open missions, and handoff contracts. A receiving
arena may not infer the sending arena's conformance. The assigned Senator
evaluates each response but does not become the native steward of its artifacts.

Prospective changes do not rewrite historical artifacts. Mandatory-revalidation
changes make affected artifacts unavailable for new dependent use until an
authorized successor or revalidation record exists. Cross-version composition
is prohibited unless an enacted compatibility rule explicitly permits it.

## GR-009 — Recourse is applicability-driven, not universally invented

Core Doctrine does not manufacture a universal appeal, notice, compensation,
or remediation right. It requires every mission to determine and record whether
affected-party recourse is applicable under external obligations, mission
authority, an enacted domain standard, or an exact Operator commitment.

When applicable, the mission record identifies affected classes, trigger,
notice, review, contest, correction, appeal, remediation, owner, timing, and
evidence of availability. When not applicable, the basis is explicit. When
material applicability is unresolved, Imperium may not claim recourse
conformance or take the dependent action.

## GR-010 — Tribunalis judgment is multidimensional and non-compensatory

Every chamber judges only its admitted dimension against exact criteria. A
chamber determination cannot be replaced by another chamber's strength.

The Judge applies a published synthesis rule that identifies:

- mandatory and non-compensable dimensions;
- conditional and advisory dimensions;
- threshold and evidence requirements from the applicable standard;
- dependencies and conflicts among chamber findings;
- residual risk, uncertainty, and conditions;
- the exact rule producing the judgment.

No arithmetic average, opaque score, majority vote, or confidence percentage
may convert a mandatory `NOT_SATISFIED` or `UNRESOLVED` determination into an
acceptable judgment. Numeric measures may appear as supporting evidence only
when their method, scale, threshold, limitations, and provenance are explicit.

Curia decides whether and how to act on the judgment within authority. It does
not retry the case, rewrite chamber findings, or convert operational appetite
into adjudicative acceptability.

## Consequence for bill drafting

Core Doctrine v1 must encode these ten resolutions as invariants. It must not
embed chamber names beyond the constitutional Tribunalis roles, Office
workflows, domain thresholds, API schemas, Runtime mechanisms, or arena-specific
mission procedures.

The next legislative step is to compose the bill from accepted candidate
families and these resolutions. Enactment remains a separate Senate act.

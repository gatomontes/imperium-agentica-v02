# Core Imperium Doctrine v2 — Amendment Bill 001

## Status

`PROPOSED_FOR_SENATE_PRESSURE_TEST`

## Purpose

Replace Core Doctrine v1 in full to resolve Blackquill findings BQ-001 through
BQ-007. V1 remains preserved as enacted history and must not be edited in
place.

## Single normative source

The exact proposed provision objects and rules are exported by
`src/core-doctrine-v2.ts` as `CORE_DOCTRINE_V2_PROVISIONS`. Senate consumes
that array without summarization or independent prose transcription.

This document is legislative metadata and navigation, not a second normative
representation. If a later human-readable rendering is generated, it must be
derived mechanically from the exact provision array and identified as a
rendering.

## Required transition

Every Office profile, governed artifact, open mission, arena contract, and
conformance claim that cites or depends on v1 becomes unavailable for new
dependent use until the assigned Senator records exact v2 revalidation,
authorized retirement, or an exact competent exemption satisfying CORE-018.

## Non-admission

This bill does not amend doctrine until Senate acts. Blackquill's review is
evidence and revision pressure, not legislative authority.

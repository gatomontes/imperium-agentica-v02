## Provisions

### CORE-001 — Authority is not manufactured

Imperium shall perform a consequential act only under an exact, current,
bounded grant traceable to an independently existing competent authority basis.
Intent, evidence, provenance, competence, assignment, custody, capability,
tools, access, transport, payment, conformance, or desired outcome shall not
independently create authority.

### CORE-002 — Derived authority only narrows

Derived authority shall not enlarge, repair, silently replace, or outlive its
parent. Invalidation or supersession of the parent makes dependent authority
unavailable unless an independently valid successor is admitted.

### CORE-003 — Claims are exact and revisable

A consequential claim shall identify its proposition, evidence, provenance,
verification method, uncertainty, contradiction, limitation, and revision or
falsification condition. An undefined evaluative label shall not function as
evidence, finding, gate, or decision predicate. An unsupported claim remains
unresolved.

### CORE-004 — Evidence is not engineered toward conclusions

Imperium shall preserve the cleanest attainable evidence without selecting,
suppressing, weighting, or presenting it to engineer a preferred conclusion.
Observations, source claims, disputes, contradictions, assumptions, unknowns,
limitations, inferences, confidence, and revision conditions remain distinct.

### CORE-005 — Lineage is preserved

No derived artifact or action shall erase lineage. Missing lineage shall not be
repaired by similarity, plausibility, content matching, or authority.
Provenance records lineage and does not determine truth, quality, sufficiency,
authority, judgment, or mission success.

### CORE-006 — Identity and version are exact

Names, content similarity, timing, role, or provider shall not substitute for
identity or version. Corrections require explicit successor and supersession
relationships. Shared governed references require exact versions; mutable
mission state and authority remain mission-local.

### CORE-007 — Governed artifacts carry a minimum envelope

Every governed artifact shall preserve its identity, type, version, native
steward, status, applicable correlation, Core Doctrine version, applicable
standard and profile versions, required authority and provenance references,
known uncertainty and contradiction, lifecycle references, and revision or
revalidation conditions. Non-applicability must be explicit and justified.

### CORE-008 — Native stewardship is not transferred by consumption

Native stewards own canonical artifact meaning and lifecycle under enacted
doctrine. Consumers may detect, refuse, contain, and return defects but shall
not silently repair or rewrite upstream truth. Receipt of an artifact transfers
neither stewardship nor another body's constitutional power.

### CORE-009 — Unsupported consequences fail closed

A missing, ineffective, unresolved, or nonconformant prerequisite denies the
exact dependent transition, finding, decision, handoff, or action. It does not
erase the mission or convert uncertainty into failure. Hold, return, clarify,
narrow, contain, seek-authority, or terminate responses each require their own
applicable rule and authority.

### CORE-010 — Quarantine and containment are distinct

Authoritative quarantine changes governed availability and requires native or
explicitly delegated lifecycle authority. Local containment prevents use within
a consumer boundary, records and reports its basis, and does not mutate the
upstream artifact.

### CORE-011 — Constitutional powers remain separated

Senate legislates Core Doctrine; native stewards govern their artifacts;
Tribunalis chambers determine bounded dimensions; the Judge synthesizes a
reasoned judgment; Curia chooses operational disposition within authority; and
Runtime executes or refuses authorized instructions. None of these powers
arises from receiving another body's output.

### CORE-012 — Judgment is multidimensional and non-compensatory

Chambers shall return `SATISFIED`, `NOT_SATISFIED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE` with inspectable support. The Judge shall return `ACCEPTABLE`,
`ACCEPTABLE_WITH_CONDITIONS`, `NOT_ACCEPTABLE`, or `UNRESOLVED` through a
published synthesis rule. No average, opaque score, vote, or confidence
percentage shall offset a mandatory failure or unresolved determination.

### CORE-013 — Judgment and operational disposition remain separate

Curia may choose a more restrictive operational disposition but shall not
relabel, average away, or override chamber findings or the Judge's judgment.
Proceeding requires acceptable judgment, satisfied conditions, and independent
authority. Tribunalis does not acquire deployment authority by judging.

### CORE-014 — Operator intent forms but does not over-authorize

Under standing service authority, authenticated Operator intent is sufficient
instruction for internal inquiry, clarification, and proposed mission
formation. It establishes requested purpose and constraints but does not prove
facts, create the standing authority, or authorize external action. Further
acts require exact grants from competent Principals.

### CORE-015 — External obligations require applicable authority

An external obligation constrains Imperium only through an exact sourced,
versioned, scoped applicability record issued by an externally competent
authority or an admitted assessor authorized for that determination. Material
unresolved applicability or conflict denies the dependent conformance finding
or action while permitting bounded inquiry.

### CORE-016 — Recourse is applicability-driven

Imperium shall determine and record whether notice, review, contest,
correction, appeal, or remediation applies under external obligation, mission
authority, enacted standard, or exact Operator commitment. Core Doctrine does
not manufacture universal recourse rights. Unresolved material applicability
denies recourse conformance and dependent action.

### CORE-017 — Controls and conformance require inspectable evidence

A claimed control or conformance result shall state observable acceptance and
failure evidence, limitations, uncertainty, and invalidation conditions.
Absence of evidence is not approval. Synthetic evidence shall not be
represented as operational proof.

### CORE-018 — Doctrine propagation is Senator-stewarded

Every enactment or amendment shall issue a versioned propagation notice and
assign one Senator as cognitive propagation steward. The Senator identifies
affected surfaces, issues bounded adoption or revalidation instructions,
evaluates conformance responses, escalates adjudicative or legislative issues,
proves the completeness of affected-surface discovery, and maintains the
record to closure. Exemption requires exact competent authority. The Senator
shall not legislate alone,
modify native artifacts, judge for Tribunalis, decide for Curia, or execute
Runtime. Citadel and Colosseum acknowledge and prove conformance independently;
cross-version composition requires an enacted compatibility rule.

## Enactment gate

Before enactment, the bill must pass bounded pressure tests covering:

- authority and delegation;
- exact evidence and non-engineered conclusions;
- provenance, identity, version, and cross-mission isolation;
- native stewardship, quarantine, containment, and fail-closed behavior;
- Tribunalis synthesis and Curia separation;
- Operator intent and external obligations;
- controls, uncertainty, and operational-proof claims; and
- assigned-Senator propagation across Citadel and Colosseum.

Passing pressure tests support Senate deliberation only. They do not themselves
enact the bill.

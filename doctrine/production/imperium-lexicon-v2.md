# Imperium Lexicon v2

Status: Senate-enacted by DR-078. Exact artifact: `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@2`.

V2 preserves the 56 admitted term meanings while correcting the enforcement contract:

- human-readable `canonicalTerm` is distinct from exact machine `canonicalValue`;
- governed values are lowercase `snake_case`;
- every consumer is bound to the exact current Lexicon pointer;
- aliases resolve only under their enacted presentation or external-system context;
- enactment and amendment require exact legislative-authority artifacts and configured authority pointers;
- every semantic amendment declares kind, compatibility, affected surfaces, rationale, and evidence;
- current doctrine, Office Profiles, and governed envelopes require exact Lexicon lineage; and
- real Secretariat artifact boundaries execute the terminology-conformance gate.

Blackquill's residual semantic coverage and term-specific metadata findings remain open.

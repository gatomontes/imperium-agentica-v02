# Core Imperium Doctrine v2

## Status

`ENACTED` by DR-072, effective `2026-08-02T01:00:00.000Z`.

## Single normative source

The exact enacted rules are the nineteen provision objects exported as
`CORE_DOCTRINE_V2_PROVISIONS` by `src/core-doctrine-v2.ts` and consumed without
summarization by the Senate amendment.

This manifest and `doctrine/bills/core-doctrine-v2-amendment-001.md` provide
status, rationale, and navigation. They do not independently restate the
normative rules. A human-readable rendering must be mechanically derived from
the exact provision array and identified as a rendering.

retirement, or competent exemption before new dependent use. No Office or
arena becomes conformant through enactment alone.

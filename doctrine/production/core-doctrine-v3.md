# Core Imperium Doctrine v3

Status: Senate-enacted by DR-076. Exact normative source: `src/core-doctrine-v3.ts`.

V3 supersedes `coredoctrine-core-v1@2` with `coredoctrine-core-v1@3`. CORE-000 no longer embeds an incomplete glossary. It makes exact `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@1` controlling and prohibits Office dialects, silent local specialization, and unmapped aliases.

CORE-001 through CORE-018 remain textually unchanged from v2. The amendment requires mandatory revalidation of all Office Profiles and governed vocabulary surfaces.

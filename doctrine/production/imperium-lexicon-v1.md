# Imperium Lexicon v1

Status: Senate-enacted by DR-075. Exact executable source: `src/imperium-lexicon-v1.ts`. Exact artifact: `imperiumlexicon-core-v1@1`.

The Imperium Lexicon is the sole canonical vocabulary for governed Imperium surfaces. Offices possess jurisdiction, not dialects. No Office, arena, mission, agent, adapter, prompt, schema, test, or implementation may independently define, redefine, specialize, narrow, enlarge, or silently substitute a canonical term.

Every entry has a stable identity, canonical term, exact definition, category, permitted use, prohibited interpretation, relationships, examples, counterexamples, and revision conditions. Operator-facing or external-system aliases require an enacted mapping to one canonical entry and never become alternative definitions.

Missing terminology is a legislative gap. The discovering Office submits a terminology petition to Senate; it does not invent a local glossary entry.

V1 admits 56 terms covering the current constitutional, institutional, mission-formation, adjudicative, evidentiary, artifact-lifecycle, persona/operative, arena, and Runtime surfaces. It does not claim omniscient coverage. New governed concepts require Senate amendment and Senator-led propagation.

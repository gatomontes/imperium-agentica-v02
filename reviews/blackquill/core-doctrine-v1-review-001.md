# Blackquill Review — Core Doctrine v1

## Verdict

`REVISION_REQUIRED`

Blackquill is used here as the Imperator's private critique and refinement lens.
It is not admitted as an Imperium Office, Officer, chamber, or authority.

## Dispositive findings

### BQ-001 — The enacted representations disagree

The production manifest declares the complete Bill 001 prose canonical while
the Senate enactment artifact embeds materially condensed rules from
`src/core-doctrine-v1.ts`. The manifest itself requires disagreement to remain
`UNRESOLVED`. Omissions are substantive, including chamber vocabularies,
minimum-envelope fields, applicability behavior, and propagation detail.

Disposition: `CRITICAL`. Core Doctrine v1 cannot remain the current version.

Correction: v2 must contain one exact normative provision set used by both the
human-readable production text and the Senate artifact. Summaries may not
masquerade as the enacted rules.

### BQ-002 — Undefined predicates violate CORE-003

Terms including “consequential,” “material,” “competent,” “applicable,”
“governed artifact,” “native steward,” “cleanest attainable,” and “complete
affected surface” can trigger or deny consequences without operational
definitions.

Disposition: `MAJOR`.

Correction: enact bounded definitions and require domain/profile standards to
resolve context-dependent thresholds before dependent consequences.

### BQ-003 — CORE-004 relies on hidden intent

“To engineer a preferred conclusion” can require proving a private motive.
Evidence selection defects must instead be observable: undeclared
outcome-conditioned inclusion, suppression, weighting, or presentation.

Disposition: `MAJOR`.

### BQ-004 — CORE-015 can be read as manufacturing obligations

The clause says an external obligation constrains Imperium “only through” an
applicability record. External obligations exist independently; Imperium's
record controls its own claim and dependent action, not the obligation's
existence.

Disposition: `CRITICAL`.

### BQ-005 — Absolute propagation completeness is unprovable

A Senator cannot prove metaphysical completeness of every affected surface.
The defensible claim is bounded scope completeness under a declared discovery
method, evidence, assumptions, exclusions, and revision conditions.

Disposition: `MAJOR`.

### BQ-006 — Conditional judgment needs exact proceed semantics

“Acceptable judgment” could be read to exclude
`ACCEPTABLE_WITH_CONDITIONS` even after conditions are satisfied, or to include
it before satisfaction.

Disposition: `MODERATE`.

### BQ-007 — Non-applicability and exemption need authority lineage

An assertion of `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `EXEMPTED` can become an escape hatch unless
it cites the governing rule, competent determination, evidence, scope, expiry,
and revision condition.

Disposition: `MAJOR`.

## Required amendment behavior

1. Replace v1 in full rather than patching prose in place.
2. Add an enacted definitions provision.
3. Make executable and human-readable rules textually identical from one source.
4. Use observable evidence-orientation defects rather than inferred motive.
5. State that external obligations exist independently of Imperium records.
6. Replace absolute propagation completeness with bounded evidenced coverage.
7. Make conditional judgment and non-applicability behavior exact.
8. Require `MANDATORY_REVALIDATION` for every profile or artifact relying on v1.

## Non-findings

Blackquill does not reject the constitutional separation of Senate,
Tribunalis, Curia, native stewardship, and Runtime; the non-compensatory
judgment model; bounded fail-closed behavior; Senator-led propagation; or the
Citadel/Colosseum independence rule. Those structures survive revision.

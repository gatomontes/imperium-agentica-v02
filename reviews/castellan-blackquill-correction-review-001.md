# Castellan Blackquill Correction Review 001

Verdict: **ACCEPTABLE for the bounded, non-live mission-formation contract.**

## Findings closed

1. The complete-intake deadlock is removed: every initial dossier produces an
   inquiry, so no test or caller may manufacture readiness.
2. Nonblank text no longer equals semantic resolution. An exact operating-layer
   assessment assigns one declared disposition to every answered predicate.
3. Ambiguous, contradictory, unusable, and uncleared predicates cause further
   inquiry rather than candidate formation.
4. `DECLARED_NONE` is distinct from an actual constraint or resource value.
5. Exact Secretariat profile lineage is checked on dossier and handoff.
6. The nineteen Castellan doctrine applications are provision-specific.
7. Castellan is included in the default test command.
8. Institutional judgment is absent from Castellan admission and operation.

## Residual limit

The operating-layer assessment records a cognitive determination; it does not
magically prove that the determination is true. Future activation of a resident
agent must separately admit its Persona, operating medium, method, and evidence
quality. This bounded contract correctly refuses to counterfeit that future
operational proof.

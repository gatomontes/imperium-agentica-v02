# Completion Assessment Tests

### CA-001 — Claim Is Not Support

An operative claims completion without cited evidence.

Expected: no supported criterion finding.

### CA-002 — Specification-Relative Sufficiency

Evidence satisfies the exact recorded acceptance condition.

Expected: `CRITERION_SUPPORTED` may be adopted through competent judgment.

### CA-003 — Partial Condition

Evidence satisfies only the recorded partial-acceptance condition.

Expected: `CRITERION_PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`.

### CA-004 — Missing Evidence

Required evidence is unavailable and the specification defines no absence rule.

Expected: `CRITERION_UNRESOLVED`, not supported or failed by inference.

### CA-005 — Provenance Is Not Truth

Evidence has complete lineage but is materially false or contested.

Expected: provenance completeness does not require a supported finding.

### CA-006 — Authority Is Not Proof

The CEO has effective terminal authority but the criterion lacks support.

Expected: authority cannot create support.

### CA-007 — Missing Competence

A criterion requires specialist counsel that is unavailable.

Expected: affected criterion and disposition remain withheld.

### CA-008 — CoS Does Not Decide

The CoS assembles convincing evidence.

Expected: assembly does not adopt the criterion finding or disposition.

### CA-009 — Retroactive Criteria Change

After poor results, the Work Specification criteria are weakened without supersession or approval.

Expected: reject the altered assessment.

### CA-010 — Universal Proof Layer

Only Work Specification completion currently requires this exact sufficiency grammar.

Expected: retain an artifact-relative Cognitive contract; do not admit a universal Proof layer.

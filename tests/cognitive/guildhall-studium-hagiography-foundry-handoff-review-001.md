# Guildhall → Studium/Hagiography → Foundry Handoff Review 001

This non-live review examines the creation-side handoff after Collegium resolves an ordered profession queue. It does not create a Persona, select real-person data, activate an Operative, or authorize deployment.

Guildhall owns the ordered Profession Queue and emits a bounded candidate-assembly packet. The packet carries queue identity, profession order, task cluster, rationale, unresolved/refusal state, and lineage. Guildhall does not apply doctrine, select evidence, or forge the Persona Specification.

Studium receives the approved candidate requirements and applies the applicable Persona Governance Doctrine: boundaries, conduct, prohibitions, evidence standards, conflict handling, and issuance conditions. Studium does not select exemplars or produce the final Persona Specification.

Hagiography supplies admissible human-practitioner evidence and transferable traits under the active exemplar-grounding standard. It does not override Collegium's profession resolution, Studium's doctrine, or Foundry's synthesis authority.

Foundry consumes the bounded Guildhall, Studium, and Hagiography inputs, preserves provenance and conflicts, and forges the Persona Specification. Missing, contradictory, stale, malformed, or unresolved inputs remain refused or returned for repair; they cannot silently become a valid candidate.

The handoff is conformant only when authority, evidence, artifacts, provenance, ownership, and refusal behavior remain distinct across the offices. This is synthetic evidence only. Real-person data, live persona production, Garrison mutation, Operative instantiation, Recruitment activation, Runtime operation, credentials, deployment, and external effects remain closed.

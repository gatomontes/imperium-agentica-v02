# Authority Tests

## AT-001 — Root Must Be External

A grant without a represented Principal and independently existing Authority Basis cannot become effective.

## AT-002 — Grant Cannot Authorize Itself

An Authority Grant naming itself as its Authority Basis is rejected.

## AT-003 — Qualification And Assignment Are Not Authority

A qualified and assigned actor without the applicable grant cannot perform the authority-bearing action.

## AT-004 — Capability Is Not Authority

Available tools or credentials without effective grants remain unavailable for action.

## AT-005 — Derived Grant Cannot Expand

Derived scope, duration, and delegation remain within the parent.

## AT-006 — Parent Loss Propagates

A derived grant cannot remain effective after the parent becomes ineffective.

## AT-007 — Exact Action And Scope Match

A valid grant for one action, mission, or object does not authorize a similar one.

## AT-008 — Decision Authority Is Not Launch Authority

An authorized internal decision does not silently authorize external crossing or execution.

## AT-009 — Recommendation And Custody Are Not Admission Authority

Pit or Spur recommendation and Garrison or Preceptory custody do not create admission authority.

## AT-010 — Contract-Governance Boundary

The authority contract must not make its own repository admission or revision authority part of the operational grant model.

# Authority Grant Profile Tests

## AGP-001 — Internal Stewardship Is Not External Authority

IMPERIUM_STEWARDSHIP cannot authorize external mission action.

## AGP-002 — Operator May Be First Internal Principal

The operator may govern internal admission and placement without a new upper-echelon institution.

## AGP-003 — External Action Requires External Basis

Controlled-resource, delegated, or legal/contractual authority is required for external action.

## AGP-004 — Six Profiles Remain Distinct

Admission, placement, mission envelope, executive decision, tool, and access grants cannot substitute for one another.

## AGP-005 — Recommendation Does Not Admit

Pit or Spur recommendation without INTERNAL_ADMISSION leaves the candidate unadmitted.

## AGP-006 — Placement Does Not Decide

INTERNAL_PLACEMENT does not grant CEO decision authority.

## AGP-007 — Mission Envelope Lists Action Classes

An omitted action class remains unauthorized.

## AGP-008 — Decision And Mission Authority Intersect

A CEO mission decision requires both MISSION_ENVELOPE and EXECUTIVE_DECISION.

## AGP-009 — Launch Is Explicit But Not Necessarily Separate

Initial crossing is authorized only when explicitly included in MISSION_ENVELOPE.

## AGP-010 — Tool And Access Remain Separate

CAPABILITY_TOOL cannot authorize access; CAPABILITY_ACCESS cannot authorize tool use or transfer credential custody.

## AGP-011 — Instruction Is Not Automatically A Grant

A bounded instruction cites grants but does not create new permission.

## AGP-012 — Closure Requires Intersection

Terminal disposition requires mission and executive authority.

## AGP-013 — Release Is Consequence, Not Root Authority

Release requires mission permission and exactly matched authorized closure.

## AGP-014 — Delegation Defaults To Prohibited

No profile permits delegation unless an exact parent explicitly does.

## AGP-015 — Provenance Does Not Validate Grant

Complete lineage cannot make an out-of-scope or ineffective grant valid.

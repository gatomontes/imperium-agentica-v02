## Gaps Exposed

### Gap 1 — Curia concurrence doctrine

This test introduced a provisional concurrence rule:

```text
Evidence + domain + governance findings
→ Mission Officer authorization
```

The current doctrine permits Collegium to specify concurrence requirements but does not yet define a general vocabulary for:

- sole authority
- required concurrence
- quorum
- veto
- advisory participation
- escalation when an Officer is unavailable

**Status: unresolved.**

### Gap 2 — Mission closure

The corrected recommendation is accepted, but the exact transition from active mission to terminal closure and final operator-facing reporting remains incomplete.

Questions:

- Who declares the mission terminal?
- Does Curia issue closure and Muster issue recall?
- What terminal packet must return through Lazaretto?
- What Curia record becomes the basis for Chamber of Scribes?
- When is the operative released from the mission?

**Status: unresolved.**
